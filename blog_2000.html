🦆 Duck News — 30 September 2000

1 duck
Week 22 · 30 Sep 2000
The season's final fixture, and the Duck Club still found time for one last piece of business before the covers go on for winter. 1st XI v Bidborough CC (home) Batting at six, Stuart Cheesman fell to a smart take by Rob Pritchard off the bowling of S Browning — the sole duck recorded this round, and a fittingly quiet way for the campaign to sign off. It's their first of the 2000 season, and the 29th of an increasingly distinguished career spanning 230 innings, good enough for 31st on the all-time list of 1261. Position six is nothing new for them either — they're already enshrined in the exclusive Full House club, having contrived a duck at every single position from 1 to 11, a feat of comprehensive non-scoring few will ever match. They're also a card-carrying member of the Debutante Duck fraternity from a first innings long since consigned to history, and a two-time Duck of the Year, back in 1994 and 1996, when this sort of thing was still a novelty rather than a habit. It clearly runs in the family too — their parent Fred Cheesman has amassed 34 career ducks of their own, and sibling Steve isn't far behind on 13, a proper household tradition of walking back without troubling the scorers. Fittingly, on this very day back in 1956, Basil Sears also made a duck against Linton Park CC, part of a 1947–1975 career that yielded 33 ducks and 24th place in the all-time rankings — the calendar clearly has a soft spot for late-September noughts. Week in summary One match, one duck, and a tidy full stop on a season that's given the club plenty to celebrate in its own peculiar way. Quality over quantity this week — sometimes the exclusive zero club prefers a single, well-crafted dismissal to a rabble. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Stewart Morrison — 5 ducks, 28 innings, 17.86% - Richard Ellis — 4 ducks, 20 innings, 20.0% - Daniel Thirkell — 4 ducks, 26 innings, 15.38% - Richard Piper — 4 ducks, 26 innings, 15.38% - Trevor Back — 3 ducks, 18 innings, 16.67% Stewart Morrison holds the crown as the campaign draws to a close, but with Richard Ellis's ominous 20% strike rate lurking, nothing is settled until the last bail's off. See you all again come the new season — the ducks never truly stop, they just migrate for winter. 🍩🏏0️⃣

🦆 Duck Report — Weekend of 23-24 September 2000

3 ducks
Week 21 · 23 Sep 2000 – 24 Sep 2000
Two fixtures on the books this weekend and the exclusive zero club welcomed three new entries, all arriving via the same unfortunate route: the 2nd XI's away day at Elliotts CC. A place of dubious distinction indeed. 🏏 Saturday — 2nd XI away to Elliotts CC The rot set in at four, where Simon Thirkell edged behind, caught Gary Buckle off Alan Ricketts. His second duck of the season and 37th of an increasingly storied career, vaulting him to 17th in the all-time charts. Simon is a paid-up member of the "50 Before a 50" club — 59 career ducks and still chasing that elusive half-century — and sits proudly among the Duck of the Year alumni of 1983, as well as the Ducks on the Road and Tourist Duck top tens, both entirely apt given today's postcode. Keeping it in the family, on this very same calendar day back in 1972 his own brother Tim Thirkell also strolled back for nought, against West Farleigh CC — the Thirkell name being something of a duck dynasty, with siblings Tim (68) and Nigel (55) and a small poultry farm of relatives besides. One ball later, at five, Trevor Back joined him, caught Max Wasson off the same relentless Ricketts. His third of the season lifts him to 55 career ducks, good for 7th on the all-time list — a rarefied altitude occupied by very few. Trevor holds down spots on the Ducks on the Road and Homebody Duck top tens simultaneously, a masterclass in consistency of a very particular kind, home or away. He too finds company in Tim Thirkell's nought of 23rd September 1972 against West Farleigh CC — evidently a fine day for ducks in Linton Park history. The collapse continued at seven as Dave Todd was trapped lbw by Phil Patterson, his second duck of the season and 15th of his career, moving to 58th in the all-time standings. And yes, on this same date in 1972, Tim Thirkell was once again making a nought of himself against West Farleigh — the man really did have a thing for late September. 📅 Week in Summary Three ducks from two matches, all concentrated in one unforgiving over-and-a-bit at Elliotts CC — Alan Ricketts claiming two of the three scalps in successive balls, a fine display of triumph through adversity from the Duck Club's perspective if not the batting order's. Sunday's fixture, mercifully, passed without incident. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Stewart Morrison — 5 ducks in 28 innings (17.86%) - Richard Ellis — 4 ducks in 20 innings (20.0%) - Daniel Thirkell — 4 ducks in 25 innings (16.0%) - Richard Piper — 4 ducks in 25 innings (16.0%) - Trevor Back — 3 ducks in 18 innings (16.67%) Three names within touching distance at the top, with the season drawing in and the race for the crown very much alive. Roll on next weekend — the ducks, as ever, will be watching for their chance.

🦆 Duck Report — Cornwall Tour Week, 26 Aug – 1 Sep 2000

4 ducks
Week 17 · 26 Aug 2000 – 1 Sep 2000
Eight matches down on the Tour XI's swing through Cornwall, and the zero club found four new inductees along the way. Not a vintage haul by touring standards, but quality over quantity, as ever. Tour XI Daniel Thirkell got the touring party's account open at Helston CC on the 29th, lbw to W Ahmed for his 3rd duck of the season and 14th of an eminent career — good enough for 62nd on the all-time list and a paid-up member of the Tourist Duck top 10. Eerily, on this very same date back in 1954 Stan Wratten (1943–1984, 35 ducks, 20th all-time) was also trudging off scoreless in Cornwall, proof the county has form for this sort of thing. Two days later at Falmouth CC, Mike Elworthy fell to A Hutton without troubling the scorers — his 2nd duck of the season and 33rd of a career now well past 330 innings. Allan Cooper (1971–1989, 38 ducks, 15th all-time) suffered the identical fate on this exact date in 1980. Mike wasn't finished there, though: 24 hours later at Mullion CC he was caught off Mark Jennings for another blank, his 3rd of the season and 34th career duck — a grim pair for the tour, Falmouth to Mullion inside a day. Tim Thirkell himself (68 ducks, 4th all-time) marked this same calendar date with a duck of his own back in 1973, so Mike keeps illustrious company. Also undone at Mullion was Christopher Thirkell, run out for nought at number nine — his 1st duck of the season and 2nd of a career just nine innings young, edging him ever closer to completing the Full House nearly-man set. Tim Thirkell again provides the on-this-day echo from 1973 — clearly a vintage year for the surname. The Thirkell name had a busy week all told, with Daniel and Christopher both visiting the club within three days of one another — kin, if not quite a joint innings. Week in Summary Four ducks from eight matches, every one of them a touring casualty, with the Thirkell clan doing more than their share of the damage. Mike Elworthy's Falmouth-to-Mullion double was the week's standout — a proper grim pair, no royal honours attached, but a fine addition to the tour scrapbook regardless. Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard - Richard Ellis — 4 ducks / 18 inn (22.22%) - Richard Piper — 4 ducks / 21 inn (19.05%) - Stewart Morrison — 4 ducks / 25 inn (16.00%) - Daniel Thirkell — 3 ducks / 23 inn (13.04%) - Mike Elworthy — 3 ducks / 23 inn (13.04%) The tour rolls on and the season isn't done with any of them yet.
