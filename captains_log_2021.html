🗓️ 27 Nov – 03 Dec 2021 · 2021 — 1 match

Sun 28 Nov 2021 🧢 Friendly XIv Bearsted CC · HomeformLWWLW

Sunday 28 November brought Bearsted CC to Linton Park on an afternoon that could have chilled the enthusiasm out of a polar bear – grey skies, a wind that seemed to have a personal vendetta against anyone attempting a cover drive, and a temperature that had half the fielding side wondering why cricket in November had ever seemed like a good idea.

None of that troubled the bat, mind you. We piled on 107 for the loss of just two wickets from a brisk nine overs, with Aaron Hughes leading the charge on 26 not out. I chipped in with 26 of my own alongside him, and Luke Welsman kept the scoreboard ticking nicely with 25 not out. A proper flurry of boundaries to keep the blood moving in that cold.

Defending it, Anthony Karassellos was magnificent, ripping through Bearsted with 3-30, and I managed to lend a hand with 1-9. Poor Lewis Bedwell departed for a golden duck without so much as troubling the scorers, and although Christian Roberts fought hard for his 20, the visitors were bowled out for 64 in just 7.3 overs – the innings collapsing rather like a garden gazebo in that wind.

Special mention, too, for two wonderful milestones: Benjamin Austin brought up 3,000 career runs for the club, and Luke Welsman reached 4,000 – proper club stalwarts, both, and long may they keep piling them on.

We ran out winners by 43 runs. Cold hands, warm hearts, and a very good day for the club.

Sun 10 Oct 2021 🧢 1st XIv The Mote CC · AwayformLWWWL

Sunday 10 October 2021 saw us make the trip to The Mote CC, under skies that couldn't quite decide whether they were showering us with rain or contempt. Winning the toss, I had no hesitation in choosing to bat — a decision I stood by right up until the moment it started raining on my parade, metaphorically and otherwise.

Nine overs brought us 93/5, which on a good day might have felt like a platform and on this one felt like a small mercy. I top-scored with 26 not out, doing my bit before handing the baton to Luke Welsman, who chipped in nicely with 23 — the man remains in irritatingly good form, sitting third in the season's run charts with 226. Spare a thought, though, for poor Anthony Karassellos, who contributed nothing but a golden duck to proceedings; these things happen to the best of us, usually when we can least afford it.

With the ball, things started well enough — I picked up 1-17, the pick of our bowlers, for what little that's worth in the final reckoning. But the wheels well and truly came off in the fifth over, two wickets falling in the same over doing nothing to stem a stand of 53 for the first wicket that did for us. The Mote raced to 97/2 in just 7.1 overs, Matthew White and Theeshan Satkunaseelan both unbeaten on 25, Matt Sadler adding 21 for good measure.

Not our day, then — beaten by four wickets. Heads up, chins up, and back to the nets.

🗓️ 25 Sep – 01 Oct 2021 · 2021 — 1 match

Sat 25 Sep 2021 🧢 Friendly XIv SC Selling CC · AwayformWLWWL

Skipper's Log, Saturday 25 September 2021 — away at Selling CC on a warm, overcast afternoon perfect for putting one's feet up, though as it turned out we'd need every over.

I called correctly at the toss, which around these parts counts as the highlight of most seasons, and had no hesitation whatsoever in choosing to bat. Craig Sands then went and reminded everyone why: 147, three figures and no less, an innings that also carried him past Paul Gibson into 7th on the club's all-time run-scorers list and over the 16,000-run mark for his career — a fine afternoon's work by anyone's reckoning. Richard Skye (71 not out) and Anthony Karassellos (51) played handsomely in support, while poor Nolan Wilde contributed a golden duck, the sort of nothing that makes for excellent tea-time ribbing. We closed on 304/5 from our 40, a total that felt, at the time, faintly untouchable.

Selling had other ideas, replying with 301/8 from their 40 — Brad Wretham (78) doing most of the damage, well supported by Tom Hinkes (46) and Nick Myers (36 not out) — and for a good while it looked touch and go. Anthony Karassellos kept his head to finish with 2-29, backed up by Aaron Hughes (2-31) and John Karassellos (2-49), and it was he who also brought up his 100th career wicket for the club along the way. He now leads our 2021 Friendly XI wicket charts with six, as Craig does the run charts with 147.

A tense finish, but a win is a win, and by 3 runs it was a famous one. Onwards.

Sat 18 Sep 2021 🧢 Friendly XIv Linden Park CC · HomeformWLWLW

Off the toss went against us, and Linden Park made themselves comfortable at the crease on a warm, grey afternoon. Karassellos now tops our Friendly XI wicket charts and Hughes sits second in the run charts, which tells its own story. A thoroughly earned victory, and one I'll happily take the credit for from the captain's chair, even if I did precious little to deserve it.
