🦆 Duck Report — 23–24 September 2017

2 ducks
Week 23 · 23 Sep 2017 – 24 Sep 2017
A quiet week for the exclusive zero club — just two additions to the ledger across two fixtures — but as ever, quality trumps quantity in the art of not scoring. 🏏 Saturday — 1st XI at Selling CC Anton Bourdillon departed for the season's 2nd duck, caught Dominic Hamlyn bowled Tony Carter at number 9. It's his 5th career duck from 15 innings, good enough for 176th on the all-time list — and while Anton is a paid-up member of the Debutante Duck club, having famously ducked on his actual debut, this one is very much a repeat offence rather than an origin story. He now sits shoulder to shoulder with Harry Bee, who played 2008–2012 and took a far more leisurely 31 innings to accumulate the same 5 — Anton, by comparison, is getting there at quite a lick. 🌱 Sunday — 1st XI at Tenterden CC Luke Welsman was bowled by Tom Richards at number 4, his 7th duck of the season and 27th of a distinguished career spanning 125 innings — 46th on the all-time roll of dishonour. Luke arrives with previous form: Duck of the Year in 2015, in 2016, and — pointedly — in 2017 too, meaning this latest failure is less a surprise than a coronation. He's also a Full House nearly-man, having ducked in 10 of the 11 batting positions, leaving one stubborn gap to complete the set. On the career tally he draws level with Craig Sands, active 2002–2017, who needed a marathon 461 innings to reach 27 — Luke has managed the feat rather more economically, which is either a badge of honour or a cause for concern depending on your point of view. 📅 Week in Summary Two matches, two ducks, one bowled, one caught behind the stumps in the field — no pairs, no family affairs, and nobody troubling the King Pair honours this week. A restrained showing all round, though the Duck of the Year race shows no sign of cooling. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Luke Welsman — 7 ducks / 46 innings (15.22%) - Darrell Houghton-Judge — 6 ducks / 29 innings (20.69%) - Benjamin Austin — 4 ducks / 25 innings (16.00%) - Engarshal Sinnaraja — 3 ducks / 5 innings (60.00% 🍩) - Tim Wright — 3 ducks / 10 innings (30.00%) Engarshal remains the standout on pure percentage — three from five is triumph through adversity of a very particular kind.
