🦆 Duck Report — 22–23 September 2001

1 duck
Week 30 · 22 Sep 2001 – 23 Sep 2001
Three fixtures out on the paddock this weekend and, remarkably, the ducks mostly gave Linton Park a wide berth. Only one soul found his way into the exclusive zero club — but he made it count. 🏏 2nd XI v Elliotts CC (home, 22 Sept) Step forward Richard Ellis, who came to the crease at four and departed shortly after, caught and bowled by Alan Ricketts for a nought that will be filed, catalogued and quietly celebrated. It's his 1st duck of the 2001 campaign but by no means his first rodeo — this is career duck number 17 from 87 innings, good enough for 55th of 1272 in the all-time roll of dishonour. A paid-up member of the Debutante Duck society (his very first senior innings, some years back now, was also a blob) and a card-carrying Quack-to-Quack honouree besides — proof that consistency, in this club, comes in more than one flavour. Neat historical footnote: on this very day back in 1977, club immortal John Thirkell — he of 90 career ducks, still unchallenged at #1 of 1272 — also trudged off for nought, against West Kent Wanderers CC no less. Some traditions span the generations. 📅 Elsewhere The other two fixtures this weekend were disappointingly duck-free affairs — bat met ball, runs were scored, and not a single naught troubled the honours board. The Duck Club will take Richard's contribution gratefully and hope for a more generous harvest next time out. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard — 2001 1. Luke Wallond — 3 ducks / 7 innings (42.86%) 2. Simon Thirkell — 6 ducks / 15 innings (40.00%) 3. Adam Pooley — 6 ducks / 16 innings (37.50%) 4. Tim Thirkell — 4 ducks / 21 innings (19.05%) 5. Stuart Cheesman — 4 ducks / 43 innings (9.30%) Luke Wallond leads on strike rate but the chasing pack are all in the hunt as the season enters its final stretch. Plenty of time yet for someone to make a late charge for the crown. Next fixture, fresh victims. Pads on, guard taken, destiny awaits. 🦆🍩0️⃣

🦆 Duck News — Weekend of 15–16 September 2001 🦆

2 ducks
Week 29 · 15 Sep 2001 – 16 Sep 2001
A quiet weekend by Duck Club standards — just two additions to the ledger — but quality over quantity, and both carry some proper pedigree. Saturday 15 September 2nd XI v Borden CC Rob Jackson departed caught and bowled by David Hollands at number six, chalking up his 2nd duck of the season and, rather alarmingly, his 2nd career duck from only 3 innings — an eye-watering strike rate for the exclusive zero club this early in one's Linton life. Rob is already a paid-up member of the Debutante Duck board, having opened his senior account with a nought some time back, so this latest effort is very much a man returning to old habits rather than breaking new ground. He sits 302nd of 1272 in the all-time list. On this day in 1982, Trevor Hales (1981–1989) also strolled back for nought, against The Mote CC — kindred spirits across the decades. Sunday 16 September 1st XI v Northend Sunday Adam Pooley, promoted to first drop, was bowled by W. Holmes for his 6th duck of the season — and his 20th career nought from 78 innings, lifting him to 49th of 1272 all time. Adam carries genuine Duck Club aristocracy on his CV: a member of the Only Two Quacks fraternity twice over — once alongside Fred Cheesman, once alongside Paul Bowles — and a standing name on the Retirement Duck board from a past farewell innings. Today's effort also draws him level on 6 ducks for the season with Simon Thirkell atop the current Duck Hunting Season charts, though Simon still leads on percentage. The chase for Duck of the Year 2001 tightens. On this day in 1962, Richard Ashby (1962–1995) also made a duck, against Staplehurst CC — proof that mid-September has always been unkind to LPCC batting. 📊 Week in Summary Two matches, two ducks, one from each end of the Linton order — a top-four man and a middle-order debut-adjacent quacker. Modest numbers, but the season race stays very much alive. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard 1. Simon Thirkell — 6 ducks / 15 inn (40.0%) 2. Adam Pooley — 6 ducks / 16 inn (37.5%) 3. Tim Thirkell — 4 ducks / 20 inn (20.0%) 4. Stuart Cheesman — 4 ducks / 42 inn (9.52%) 5. Luke Wallond — 3 ducks / 7 inn (42.86%) Simon Thirkell clings to top spot, but Adam Pooley's Sunday effort has him breathing down his neck. With the season drawing in, there's still time for one more push toward glory — or ignominy, depending on your view. Next fixture, the Duck Club's scouts will be watching every top edge and leading edge with keen interest. Bring your bat, or don't bother — either way, we'll be here to record it. 🦆🍩

🦆 Duck News — Week of 8-12 September 2001

5 ducks
Week 28 · 8 Sep 2001 – 12 Sep 2001
Five fresh entries for the exclusive zero club this week, headlined by a rare full-family production at West Farleigh and a determined double from the ever-reliable Trevor Back. The 2001 season race for the dubious crown continues to tighten nicely. 🏏 Saturday 8th September — 2nd XI at West Farleigh CC A proper cluster on the road for the 2nd XI, three ducks in a single innings and one of them a genuine club ceremony. Simon Thirkell fell lbw to Mark Meyrick for his 6th duck of the season and 43rd of an illustrious career spanning 189 innings, good enough for 14th on the all-time list. He's already got 1983 and 2001 Duck of the Year honours to his name, sits proudly on the "50 Before a 50" board, and features among the club's great travelling quackers — on this day back in 1981, Richard Ashby (1962–1995, 28 career ducks) was doing exactly the same thing against Paul CC. Trevor Back edged behind for his 1st of the season, caught Terry Baines off John Smith, and moved to 56 career ducks, 6th on the all-time list and closing in on the very top table. Still a proud member of the Homebody, Lone Duck and Ducks on the Road top tens, and a fixture of the Only Two Quacks and Quacking Together honour boards. Same 1981 vintage as Simon above, Richard Ashby keeping him company from the archives. But the headline act was further down the order. Christopher Thirkell, caught David Crouch off Adrian Davies, brought up his 3rd of the season and 5th career duck in just 21 innings, and in doing so joined his own father Simon on the scoresheet for a textbook Duck and Ducklings double, Linton's rarest and most treasured ceremony. Two Thirkells, one innings, nought between them. Christopher also lingers agonisingly on the Full House nearly-man board, one position shy of the full set. Richard Ashby, 1981, obliges again for the parallel. 🌙 Wednesday 12th September — Midweek XI vs Lord Cornwallis XI Trevor Back was back for more, lbw to Julian Wilson this time, completing a grim pair for the week and moving to 2 for the season and 57 for his career. On this day in 1959, S Lusher (1959–1960, a single lonely career duck) was suffering the same fate against Bowaters CC. Alongside him, Tim Thirkell, brother to Saturday's Simon, departed caught behind off Julian Wilson too, his 4th of the season and 72nd of a mighty career across 738 innings, 4th on the all-time chart and a Duck of the Year 1972 alumnus with the rare Full House board, a duck at every position, to his name. Same 1959 company as Trevor, S Lusher keeping the archives ticking over. 📊 Week in Brief Five ducks in four matches, three of them arriving in one glorious West Farleigh innings. Trevor Back top of the workload with two, and the Thirkell name appearing on four of the five dismissals, a family clearly building its own wing of the honours board. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard (2001) 1. Simon Thirkell — 6 ducks, 15 innings (40.0%) 2. Adam Pooley — 5 ducks, 15 innings (33.3%) 3. Tim Thirkell — 4 ducks, 20 innings (20.0%) 4. Stuart Cheesman — 4 ducks, 40 innings (10.0%) 5. Luke Wallond — 3 ducks, 7 innings (42.9%) Simon leads the pack, but with Tim closing and the Thirkell dynasty in irrepressible form, this season's crown is very much a family affair. Roll on the next fixture, the ducks are surely circling. 🦆🏏0️⃣

🦆 Duck News — 1 September 2001

0 ducks
Week 27 · 1 Sep 2001
A quiet one this week, Duck Club faithful. One solitary fixture on the books for the period and, remarkably, not a single nought to show for it. Every batsman who walked to the crease on 1st September walked back having troubled the scorers at least once. The zero club stood at the gate all afternoon and nobody turned up to join. Rare, and frankly a little suspicious. The art of not scoring is not easily mastered and it seems nobody fancied the lesson this week — but as any seasoned member of the club will tell you, the ducks are never gone for good. They are simply biding their time, waiting for a rash shot, a good yorker, or an unplayable bit of umpiring to bring the next candidate into the fold. So no honours board entries, no milestones, no father-and-son near misses to report. Just a rare weekend of universal competence. Make the most of it. 🍩 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 🍩 1️⃣ Simon Thirkell — 5 ducks in 14 innings (35.71%) 2️⃣ Adam Pooley — 5 ducks in 15 innings (33.33%) 3️⃣ Stuart Cheesman — 4 ducks in 38 innings (10.53%) 4️⃣ Luke Wallond — 3 ducks in 7 innings (42.86%) — the division leader on pure percentage, and not a title to be proud of 5️⃣ Tim Thirkell — 3 ducks in 18 innings (16.67%) Luke Wallond's 42.86% remains the most alarming number in the division — the kind of consistency that gets you noticed for all the wrong reasons. Simon Thirkell and Adam Pooley sit level at the summit on 5 apiece, and with the season not yet done there's ample time for one of them to pull clear — or for a fresh face to come steaming through on the rails. 🏏 Onwards to the next fixture. The ducks evaded capture this week, but the traps are set and the club awaits its next inductees with open arms and a suitably solemn round of applause. 0️⃣🦆

🦆 Duck News — Tour Week, 25–31 August 2001

2 ducks
Week 26 · 25 Aug 2001 – 31 Aug 2001
Six matches went by on this touring week and the ducks proved remarkably elusive — just two additions to the rolls, both courtesy of the touring party itself. Quality, evidently, over quantity. 🏏 Tour XI Away at Paul CC on the 28th, opener Stuart Cheesman departed for nought, caught and bowled by T. Mahmood — his 4th duck of the season and 33rd of his career, a tally that now sits him a lofty 25th of 1269 in the all-time standings. Stuart is a paid-up member of the Debutante Duck club (his very first Linton innings, some years back, was itself a blob), a two-time Duck of the Year (1994 and 1996), and holder of the rare Full House honour — a duck at every position from 1 to 11, a curious sort of completeness. On this very calendar day back in 1996, Daniel Thirkell (1993–2001, 16 ducks, 56th all-time) was making his own nought against 1st Innings Mullion — the ledger, it seems, has a long memory. Ducking clearly runs in the blood too, with father Fred sat on 36 career ducks and brother Steve on 13. Three days later, away at Mullion CC, Adam Pooley was bowled by David Fowles without troubling the scorer — his 5th duck of the season and 19th of his career, good for 51st of 1269 all-time. Adam already features twice on the Only Two Quacks board, paired with Fred Cheesman and with the legendary Paul Bowles, and carries the standing Retirement Duck honour from a past farewell innings. On this same day in 1980, Allan Cooper (1971–1989, 38 ducks, 16th all-time, himself a three-time Only Two Quacks honouree) was going through precisely the same ritual at Staplehurst. 📊 Week in Summary Just two ducks from six matches — a quiet one by Linton's exacting standards. But Adam Pooley's fifth of the campaign draws him level at the summit of this year's Duck Hunting Season charts, setting up a proper scrap for the crown. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 1. Simon Thirkell — 5 ducks / 14 inns (35.71%) 2. Adam Pooley — 5 ducks / 15 inns (33.33%) 3. Stuart Cheesman — 4 ducks / 38 inns (10.53%) 4. Luke Wallond — 3 ducks / 7 inns (42.86%) 5. Tim Thirkell — 3 ducks / 18 inns (16.67%) 📅 Looking Ahead A lean week for the exclusive zero club, but the season's far from settled — with Simon and Adam neck and neck, next weekend's fixtures could crown a new leader in the art of not scoring. We watch. We wait. We celebrate. 🦆

🦆 Duck News — Week of 11–17 August 2001

4 ducks
Week 24 · 11 Aug 2001 – 17 Aug 2001
A quiet week in the Duck Hunting fields, but no week is ever entirely fowl-free at Linton Park. Four ducks fell across five fixtures, spread thin but each with a story worth telling. 1st XI Shipbourne away brought us the week's opening pair. Daniel Thirkell was run out for nought — the Thirkell name barely needs an introduction on the honours board, and this is his 16th career duck from 196 innings, planting him 56th of 1269 all-time and a paid-up member of the Tourist Duck top 10. He now sits level with 1978-2001 stalwart Dave Todd, also on 16 from 213 innings — a fine bit of company to draw level with, if company on a duck can ever be called fine. And on this very day back in 1981, Trevor Hales was doing exactly the same thing against West Moors — some traditions simply refuse to die. Also run out for nought at Shipbourne was Kevin Presneill, opening his club account in only his 3rd innings. A tidy little tally of 1 apiece with the briefly-remembered "- Copeland", who played precisely one innings in 1979 and made it count for nothing — Copeland's whole career is enshrined in the One and Done board, so Kevin has some way to go before matching that particular brand of commitment. Over at Westgate-on-Sea, Mike Redman was trapped lbw by Tom Hammond for his 2nd duck of the season and 2nd of his career from just 12 innings — the ink still drying on this one. On this day in 1970 the great Mike Iliffe was also nodding a weary walk back against Gravesend, en route to a career 43 ducks and 13th on the all-time list. Redman has a way to go, but every great career of not scoring starts somewhere. Midweek XI The Mote CC away saw Richard Piper lbw'd by Tom Parsons — his 2nd of the season and a very substantial 25th of his career from 145 innings, good enough for 38th all-time and membership of no fewer than four honours boards: Ducks on the Road, Full House, Homebody Duck and Lone Duck top 10s. A genuine club institution in the art of not scoring. Fittingly, on this very day in 1964 the legendary John Thirkell — Linton's all-time duck king with 90 to his name — was also making his way back against Bowaters CC. Good company, if you can call it that. Week in Summary Four ducks, five matches, no pairs, no family affairs this time round — just honest, workmanlike failure spread across the club. The 1st XI contributed three of the four, with the Midweek XI chipping in one of genuine pedigree. Steady as she goes. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 1. Simon Thirkell — 5 ducks, 13 innings (38.46%) 2. Adam Pooley — 4 ducks, 14 innings (28.57%) 3. Luke Wallond — 3 ducks, 7 innings (42.86%) 4. Tim Thirkell — 3 ducks, 13 innings (23.08%) 5. Stuart Cheesman — 3 ducks, 32 innings (9.38%) Luke Wallond's 42.86% strike rate for nought remains the one to watch — can anyone catch him before season's end? Get down to the next fixture and find out. The zero club never sleeps. 🦆🏏0️⃣

🦆 Duck News — 4th–5th August 2001

4 ducks
Week 23 · 4 Aug 2001 – 5 Aug 2001
A modest but perfectly formed haul this week: four ducks across three matches, proof that the art of not scoring remains alive and well at Linton Park. Saturday 4th August — Headcorn CC The exclusive zero club welcomed two new entries at Headcorn. Russell Baldwin, promoted to first drop, was trapped lbw by Mike Donnelly for his first duck of the season and 4th of an increasingly dubious career, moving to 59 innings and 167th in the all-time list. On this day in 1976, Robert Chance made a duck against Sennocke CC on his way to 8 for the club (1964-1978) — Russell keeps him company down the years. Down at number seven, Ron McCormick followed him back, caught behind off R- Dickinson. It's his maiden duck for the club in 71 innings — 547th on the all-time list, and already, per the honours board, a paid-up member of the Retirement Duck club from a past farewell. Like Russell, Ron shares his on-this-day company with Robert Chance's 1976 effort at Sennocke. Tim Thirkell then completed the Headcorn hat-trick of humility, caught by Peter West off Pete Sparks. It's Tim's 3rd duck of the season and 71st of a genuinely gargantuan career — a Full House member (duck at every position 1-11), a permanent resident of the Lone Duck, Homebody Duck and Ducks on the Road top 10s, and Duck of the Year back in 1972. The surname carries weight here: Tim's late kinsman John Thirkell tops the all-time charts outright on 90, with brothers Nigel (55) and Simon (42) also well represented. Tim, too, walks in step with Robert Chance's 1976 Sennocke special. Sunday 5th August — Downham & Bellingham CC Fred Cheesman kept the 1st XI's afternoon honest, holing out to Gary Hopper off Chris Mendham at number seven. It's Fred's 2nd of the season and 36th for the club — 19th all-time — and he arrives with a mantelpiece groaning under honours: back-to-back Duck of the Year in 1990 and 1991, the rare Full House (duck at every position), a fixture of the Tourist Duck top 10, and one half of Only Two Quacks with a certain Adam Pooley, who handily sits second in this very season's charts below. Fred's duck-collecting runs in the family too, with Stuart (32) and Steve (13) both established members. On this day in 1895, Ernest Cruttenden made a duck against Rochester CC on his way to that season's Duck of the Year — kindred spirits 106 years apart. 📊 The Week in Brief Four ducks, three matches, and Headcorn CC alone accounted for three of them. Consistency of a very particular kind. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard 1. Simon Thirkell — 5 ducks, 12 innings (41.67%) 2. Adam Pooley — 4 ducks, 12 innings (33.33%) 3. Luke Wallond — 3 ducks, 6 innings (50.0%) 4. Tim Thirkell — 3 ducks, 12 innings (25.0%) 5. Stuart Cheesman — 3 ducks, 28 innings (10.71%) A proper family affair up top, with Simon Thirkell leading and brother Tim lurking in 4th. Luke Wallond's 50% strike rate remains the most efficient failure in the club. Next fixture beckons — bring your bat, or don't bother. The Duck Club will be watching either way. 🦆🍩

Week in Summary Three ducks from four matches — two bowled, one lbw, and one fixture escaped completely unscathed. Not a vintage week for quantity, but Simon Thirkell's 5th of the season keeps the 2001 Duck of the Year race firmly alive. 🦆 Duck Club Bulletin — 14–15 July 2001

4 ducks
