🌟 Famous Faces at Linton Park

Internationals, county cricketers and celebrated guests who have turned out at Linton Park down the years — as visiting stars, invitational XIs, or opponents on their way up. Each is drawn from the club's own scorecards.

⚔️ Famous opponents at the Park

Alan Butcher

Cricket player of England. (born 1954)
Lashings World CC

Alan Raymond Butcher is a former English cricketer who is part of a family known for its strong cricketing connections. Although only selected to play for England on one occasion, he was lauded for his skills in first-class cricket and was named a Wisden Cricketer of the Year in 1991. He became Essex coach in 1993, and coached Surrey between 2005 and 2008. Cricket writer, Colin Bateman noted Butcher was, "a popular and accomplished left-handed opener, unlucky to be consigned to membership of the 'One Cap Club'... despite consistent county performances and an ability to tackle quick bowlers, Butcher was passed over".

Brent Arnel

New Zealand cricketer

Brent John Arnel is a New Zealand former professional cricketer who has played six Test matches for the national side. A fast bowler, he represented Northern Districts and Wellington in a domestic career that began in 2006.

Claire Taylor

English cricketer
Lord Cornwallis X1 - Midweek XI

Samantha Claire Taylor is a former cricketer who represented England more than 150 times between 1998 and 2011. A top order batter, Taylor was the first woman to be named a Wisden Cricketer of the Year. Along with Charlotte Edwards, she was the mainstay of England's batting during the first decade of the 21st century, and played a key role in the team's two world titles in 2009.

Clare Taylor

English sportswoman
Lord Cornwallis xi - Midweek XI

Clare Elizabeth Taylor is an English sportswoman, the first woman to have played on a World Cup team in both cricket and football. She represented England at both cricket, as a member of the winning World Cup cricket team in 1993, and football. Taylor attended Moor End Academy, her name was previously on the athletics record board but it has since been removed. Taylor was the first bowler for England to take 100 wickets in WODIs.

Grace Scrivens

England U19 Women captain / Kent Women
Faced as opponent

Grace Elizabeth Scrivens is an English cricketer who currently plays for Essex and Trent Rockets. An all-rounder, she is a left-handed batter and right-arm off break bowler. She previously played for Sunrisers.

Played age-group cricket against Linton Park for Headcorn CC and Leeds & Broomfield CC (2016–2019) on the way up.
