🗓️ 23 Sep – 29 Sep 2006 · 2006 — 2 matches

Sun 24 Sep 2006 🧢 Sunday 1st XIv TC Tenterden CC · AwayformDWDLL

Sunday, 24 September 2006, and the road took us down to Tenterden – a fixture that always promises tea worth the journey and, on this occasion, delivered rather more besides. I lost the toss, we were asked to field, and I confess my heart sank slightly at the prospect of a long afternoon in the deep. As it turned out, we needn't have worried.

Tenterden battled to 192 all out in 47.5 overs, built around a solid top order – Paul Hope's 49 and Richard Pearman's 48 doing the heavy lifting, with Fred Pearman chipping in 34. But the bowling attack had other ideas: Tom Murphy was superb, finishing with 3-21, while Michael Beeney bowled with real discipline for 3-26 including three maidens, and Thomas Beeney weighed in with 1-30. Mark Kokkinos and Paul Brabon both departed without troubling the scorers – sorry, lads, that duck emoji was for you.

Chasing 193, I was fortunate enough to find some form early and kept finding the gaps all the way to 80, which I'll admit felt rather nice after a season of more modest returns. Richard Piper matched me stroke for stroke with a well-crafted 67, and between us we saw the side home at 195/5 in 38.2 overs, a five-wicket win.

Two points, thoroughly earned, and a reminder of why we keep making that trip to Tenterden – for afternoons exactly like this one.

Beaten by 68 runs, then, and no arguing with the scoreboard. Still, with 264 runs on the season I find myself sat fourth in the run charts, which is some small comfort on the drive home. We'll dust ourselves down and be better for it next week.

Ninety-one runs the wrong side of the ledger, then. Chins up, boots cleaned, back to the nets — Sands's landmark is the one bright thread to carry into next week.
