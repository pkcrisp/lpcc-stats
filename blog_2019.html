🦆 Duck Report — Weekend of 21-22 September 2019

1 duck
Week 23 · 21 Sep 2019 – 22 Sep 2019
Two fixtures out on the park this weekend, and the hunting party returned with just a single bird bagged — a quiet one by Duck Club standards, but this lone quack carries some proper pedigree. Sunday 1st XI — away at Tenterden CC Opening the innings and evidently in a giving mood, Mark Christmas departed early — ct Tom Richards b Harry Karim — for his 3rd duck of the season and 37th of a genuinely distinguished career. That plants him 27th on the all-time roll of 1,496 duck-collectors, a career built on consistency of a very particular kind. Mark remains a card-carrying member of the Duck of the Year honours board courtesy of his 2008 campaign, and sits agonisingly close to completing the club's rarest set — the Full House nearly-man, having now ducked in 10 of the 11 batting positions. One slot left. The suspense is unbearable. A pleasing footnote for the historians: on this very date in 1977, club great John Thirkell — 90 career ducks across a mighty 1943–1967 innings, still 3rd on the all-time table — also trudged off for nought, against West Kent Wanderers CC. Some traditions really do keep to the calendar. Week in Summary One duck from two matches — the rest of both batting line-ups kept their discipline, or their luck, largely intact. A lean harvest, but the art of not scoring rarely rests for long. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard - John Karassellos — 11 ducks / 24 innings (45.83%) - Darrell Houghton-Judge — 5 ducks / 35 innings (14.29%) - Anton Bourdillon — 4 ducks / 8 innings (50.00%) 🍩 - Jordan Austin — 4 ducks / 31 innings (12.90%) - Aaron Hughes — 4 ducks / 37 innings (10.81%) John Karassellos leads the chase for Duck of the Year, but Anton Bourdillon's frankly alarming 50% strike rate keeps him lurking as the dark horse of the campaign. Next weekend brings fresh fixtures and, inevitably, fresh candidates for the exclusive zero club. Pads on, guards taken, and may the odds be ever in nobody's favour. 🏏0️⃣

🦆 DUCK NEWS — 14 to 18 September 2019

7 ducks
Week 22 · 14 Sep 2019 – 18 Sep 2019
Three fixtures, seven ducks, and proof that the exclusive zero club never quite closes for business. Cudham Wyse CC and Linton Park CC did the honours this round, with a rare family double thrown in for good measure and one repeat offender collecting a weekend pair. 🏏 Saturday 14 September — Friendly XI v Cudham Wyse CC Opener Aaron Hughes departed early, caught Inwood bowled Sharp, for his 4th duck of the season — and, remarkably, his 4th career duck, every single one arriving in 2019. He joins the class of this very day in 1958, when Stan Wratten made his own duck against Tunbridge Wells CC on his way to 35 career ducks across four decades of service. Aaron's relatives Toby and Will Hughes are already fellow paid-up members of the club, sitting on 2 and 1 respectively. Archie Fairfax-Ross followed, caught Walayat bowled Sharp, his 2nd of the season and 3rd for his career, drawing level with Daniel McCarthy (2006–07, 19 innings) in the all-time list. Then the afternoon's finest ceremony: a genuine Duck and Ducklings. Henry Bourdillon (caught Mehmood bowled Mohammad, 2nd of the season, 2nd career, level with Abishek Pushpavinothan) and parent Anton Bourdillon (caught Inwood bowled Mohammad, 4th of the season, 9th career, level with Charles Curteis) both departed for nought in the very same innings. Two generations, two ducks, one thoroughly shared shame — the rarest ceremony the club can offer, solemnly noted for the record. Anton, a long-serving member of the Debutante Duck club from his own first-ever innings years back, shows the trait plainly runs in the family. Sandwiched neatly between the Bourdillons, John Karassellos was lbw to Mohammad for his 10th duck of the season and 22nd of his career, drawing level with Alan Boyns (1986–2016, 219 innings). Reigning Duck of the Year for both 2018 and 2019, and still stalking that elusive 11th position for a Full House, Karassellos wasn't quite finished with the weekend... 🏏 Sunday 15 September — 1st XI v Linton Park CC ...because 24 hours later he was back, bowled James West, for his 11th of the season and 23rd career duck — a grim weekend pair, Saturday and Sunday back to back. On this very date in 1982, Trevor Hales made his own duck against The Mote CC; Karassellos now keeps him company in the on-this-day ledger. He wasn't dismissed alone. Ryan Silk, caught Keeler bowled West, added his 2nd of the season and 4th career duck, also sharing that same 1982 date with Trevor Hales. Ryan remains a card-carrying member of the Retirement Duck club from a past farewell, though no such drama threatened on this occasion. 📊 Week in Summary Seven ducks from three matches, headlined by a father-and-offspring special at Cudham Wyse and a Saturday-to-Sunday pair for John Karassellos, who now carries a frankly alarming 47.83% season duck rate. The art of not scoring is alive, well, and thoroughly hereditary at Linton Park. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard 1. John Karassellos — 23 inns, 11 ducks (47.83%) 2. 🦆 Duck News — 31 August & 1 September 2019

6 ducks
Week 20 · 31 Aug 2019 – 1 Sep 2019
Three matches, six ducks, and the exclusive zero club welcomed its usual cast of hopefuls this weekend. The art of not scoring was practised with real dedication on both days. A Duck of the Year winner back in 2008 and a perennial "Full House nearly-man" (10 of 11 positions ticked off), Mark keeps building a CV any duck connoisseur would envy. Craig Sands followed at six, lbw to Stephen Innes, his 3rd of the season and 31st career duck — 35th on the all-time roll. He now sits level with Joshua Knowler (2008-2019, 242 innings), the 2010 Duck of the Year and proud owner of a Diamond Duck for good measure. Last man Martin Harris rounded things off, caught by Mark Lewis-Ranwell off Miles Tucker for his 1st of the season and 30th of his career, ranking 40th all-time — level with Daniel Thirkell (1993-2019, 506 innings). Martin himself carries the 2011 Duck of the Year honour, and isn't even the leading duck-getter in his own house — parent Jon Harris has quietly amassed 94 career noughts. Sunday — 1st XI away at Stone-in-Oxney CC Jordan Austin, caught by Owen Piper off Jay Parker, chalked up his 3rd duck of the season and 26th career nought, 53rd on the all-time list. A Diamond Duck holder from 2018 and a fixture in both the Homebody and Lone Duck top tens, Jordan draws level on 26 with Darrell Houghton-Judge (2012-2019). Ducking clearly runs in the blood too — sibling Benjamin (17) and offspring Mark (1) are both paid-up club members. Sunday — 2nd XI away at Holborough Anchorians CC William Roxburgh survived six balls before falling lbw to Zabi Armani, his 1st senior duck, 715th on the all-time list — level there with the briefest of careers, "- Howard", whose single 2006 innings also yielded a Debutante Duck, a Retirement Duck, and top billing in the One and Done club. And then there was John Karassellos. Bowled by Sayad Jabarkhall for a two-ball stay, his 8th duck of the season already runs away with the 2019 Duck of the Year race — a title he also claimed in 2018 — while his 20th career duck extends a run of five in a row and keeps his Full House nearly-man status warm. He stands 74th all-time, sharing this very calendar date with the club's own runaway leader Tim Thirkell, who made a duck against Whitstable CC back in 1973 en route to a barely believable 95. The Karassellos name is a duck dynasty in miniature too, with Michaela (2) and Anthony (1) both on the books. Week in summary Six ducks from three matches, every single one recorded away from home — Linton's bowlers clearly left the noughts for the road trip. No pairs, no family doubles, just honest one-at-a-time contributions to the cause. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 1. John Karassellos — 8 ducks in 19 innings (42.11%) 2.
