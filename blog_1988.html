🦆 Duck News — 1 October 1988

2 ducks
Week 24 · 1 Oct 1988
A short week on the fixture card but a productive one for the exclusive zero club — one match, two new entries, and the honours boards did not sit idle. 🏏 1st XI v Hastings & St Leonards Priory CC (home) First to fall was Paul Sendles, lbw to the mysteriously unrecorded "Unsure" at number six — cricket's way of saying even the scorer couldn't quite believe it. It's his maiden senior innings, and he departs with it: 1 duck from 1 innings, ranking 452nd of 1,110 in the all-time reckoning. That's an immediate double induction — straight onto the Debutante Duck board for a nought on arrival, and, with the ledger currently reading one innings and one duck, provisionally filed under One and Done for the Class of '88 too. Whether he stays there is entirely up to him — the club will be watching with interest rather than writing eulogies. Neat historical footnote: on this very date in 1978, Ian Webb (11 ducks, 1976–1986) was going through precisely the same ritual. Four wickets later, Stewart Morrison joined him, bowled by Andrew Phillips down at ten. It's his 2nd duck of the season and 5th of a career now standing at 19 innings — good enough for 117th all-time, level company with names spanning six decades of Linton batting misfortune. Stewart already carries the Debutante Duck badge from a duck on his own long-ago arrival, and sits agonisingly one position shy of the Full House — a duck at every batting slot from 1 to 11. Number ten today; the completists among us note he's collecting the set. And, rather charmingly, the calendar repeats itself again — Ian Webb was making his 1978 duck on this same date too, clearly a day the fixture gods reserve for ducks. 📊 Week in Summary One match, two ducks, no pairs and no family affairs to report this time round — a modest but tidy contribution to the season's tally. Both fell in the same innings against the same attack, proof that misfortune, like tea, is best served in company. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard 1. Matt Large — 5 ducks / 19 innings (26.32%) 2. 🦆 Duck News — 24 to 28 September 1988

3 ducks
Week 23 · 24 Sep 1988 – 28 Sep 1988
Three fixtures, three ducks, and the exclusive zero club welcomes two familiar faces plus one very grand old member indeed. Saturday 24 September 1st XI at home to Malta Maniacs – Other At number five, Dave Todd was bowled by C Reynolds and duly checked back into the club for the second time this season. It's his 7th career duck from 43 innings, parking him 91st of 1109 in the all-time table — level company with A Startup (1868–1882), David Piper, J Barclay, Tony Levick and Tony Tipples, all also sat on exactly 7. Fittingly, on this very date back in 1977, club great Jim Hall was also dismissed for nought, against West Farleigh CC — Jim finished his career with 67 ducks and sits 2nd all-time, so Dave has a fair way still to travel. Wednesday 28 September Midweek XI away to Penshurst Park CC Opener Peter Whyte went early, bowled by M Latter, for his 2nd duck of the season and 4th of his career from 32 innings — 137th of 1109 all-time. On this same date in 1952, Ray Tippen also made a duck against Linton Park CC, one of the four he retired on as a paid-up Retirement Duck club member. Further down the order, number nine Jon Harris was run out for nought — a comparatively modest addition given the company he keeps. It's "only" his 1st duck of the season but his 63rd career duck, an almost unfathomable total that holds him at 3rd of 1109 all-time. Jon is a fully decorated honours-board resident — Debutante Duck, three-time Duck of the Year (1976, 1980, 1981), and proud owner of a Full House, a duck at every position from one to eleven. He shares Quacking Together honours with both Jim Hall and Trevor Back, and he's not alone in the family either — fellow duck-club member Richard Harris carries 36 career noughts of his own. Same fixture, same date, same on-this-day company as Peter — Ray Tippen, back in 1952 against Linton Park CC. Week in Summary Three ducks from three matches — the 1st XI's clash and the Midweek trip both drew blood, while a third fixture escaped entirely unplucked. Not a vintage haul, but quality over quantity: one veteran edging ever closer to the summit of the all-time charts is always worth the price of admission. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Matt Large — 5 ducks, 18 innings (27.78%) - Simon Thirkell — 3 ducks, 8 innings (37.5%) - Dave Todd — 2 ducks, 14 innings (14.29%) - Peter Whyte — 2 ducks, 14 innings (14.29%) - Alan Boyns — 2 ducks, 17 innings (11.76%) Simon Thirkell's strike rate remains the one to fear — over a third of his innings end in nought. Matt Large leads on volume, but Simon leads on pure menace. 🍩 The next fixture always has room for new arrivals to the club. Bring your bat. Or, in the spirit of the thing, don't bother. 🦆0️⃣🏏

Week 19 · 27 Aug 1988 – 2 Sep 1988
Four fixtures were on the card this week but the Duck Club found the going lean — only the touring party out at Beacon CC, Cornwall troubled the honours board, and troubled it twice in the same innings. 🏏 Tour XI — away to Beacon CC, Cornwall Simon Thirkell departed for nought batting at four, bowled by J Gerry — his third duck of the campaign and his 21st for the club in 85 innings, good enough for 36th on the all-time list. He now stands level with the "50 Before a 50" set (59 ducks await, high score a modest 48), a Duck of the Year alumnus from 1983, and a fixture of both the Ducks on the Road and Tourist Duck top tens — fitting form for a man touring Cornwall in search of precisely nothing. Quacking, it must be said, runs thick in the Thirkell bloodline, with a parent, two siblings and a cousin all paid-up members of this exclusive zero club between them. Ian is a card-carrying "Debutante Duck", a veteran of "Ducks in a Row" (seven on the trot, a feat of sustained futility), and one half of "Only Two Quacks" alongside Michael Beeney — a résumé of failure any club statistician would be proud to file. Remarkably, both men can trace their nought to the very same date in club history — on this day in 1968, twenty years to the year, Paul Bowles also strolled back for a duck, against Bearsted CC, en route to a career 45 and 7th place all-time. The calendar, it seems, has a taste for anniversaries. 📊 Week in Brief Just two ducks from four matches — a lean haul by Duck Club standards, both delivered by the touring party out west while the home sides, most unusually, kept their bats occupied throughout.
