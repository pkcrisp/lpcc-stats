🗓️ 23 Sep – 29 Sep 2023 · 2023 — 1 match

Sat 23 Sep 2023 🧢 Friendly XIv Town Malling CC · HomeformLLWDL

Saturday brought Town Malling CC to Linton Park under a grey but perfectly agreeable sky, and having won the toss I did what any captain with a scrap of sense does on a slow, grey afternoon — I stuck us in.

It came off rather nicely. Benjamin Austin and Neill Pennefather put on 90 for the first wicket before anyone troubled the umpire, and when the breakthrough finally came it barely dented us: Austin motored serenely to 51 not out, with Aaron Hughes matching him stroke for stroke on 50 not out, and Pennefather's 36 rounding out a total of 186 for 8 from our 35 overs. Only poor Euan Beardwell had cause to hang his head, back in the hutch for a duck and into the ledger for all to see.

Defending it, we bowled with real discipline. Town Malling never got away from us and eventually folded from 109 for 7 to 112 all out in 33.1 overs, Glen Baker's 29 and Lucas Dick's 24 the only real resistance. Daniel Barrett and Steve Sutton departed without scoring, Sam King bowled two tidy maidens, Laurie Probert and Alex Wade chipped in, and I had the pleasure of finishing with 2 for 25.

A thoroughly satisfying 74-run win, then — and with Aaron Hughes topping the run charts and Tim Thirkell second among the wicket-takers this season, the form guide, unlike my knees after fielding, is looking rather good.

Sun 17 Sep 2023 🧢 Friendly XIv Brockley CC, London · HomeformLLLWD

Sunday brought us Brockley CC at home, under skies that couldn't decide between sunshine and drizzle, rather like our afternoon's fortunes. I won the toss and, fancying some early swing in the damp air, put them in. It didn't quite work out as planned.

All told, a chastening 105-run defeat. Not our day, lads — heads up, and back to the nets.
