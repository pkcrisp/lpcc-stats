🦆 Duck News — 22 September 2012

0 ducks
Week 20 · 22 Sep 2012
A quiet one this week, quackers. Linton took to the field on the 22nd and — brace yourselves — not a single duck was recorded. Eleven bats went out, eleven bats found some sort of contact with the ball, and the Duck Club's talent-spotters were left twiddling their thumbs in the pavilion. The art of not scoring, it seems, took the weekend off. Rest assured this is merely a stay of execution, not a retirement notice. The ducks are out there somewhere, biding their time, and normal service of dubious distinction will resume presently. 🏏 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 1. Will Thew — 6 ducks / 31 innings (19.35%) 2. -Matthew Ward — 4 ducks / 8 innings (50.0%) 🍩 3. Ray Dixon — 4 ducks / 11 innings (36.36%) 4. Thomas Beeney — 4 ducks / 21 innings (19.05%) 5. -Adam Wright — 3 ducks / 7 innings (42.86%) Special mention to Matthew Ward, whose 50% strike rate for nought remains the most statistically alarming entry in this season's top five — a genuine coin-flip every time he walks out. Adam Wright isn't far behind either, quietly assembling a portfolio that would make any actuary nervous. Will Thew sits pretty at the summit, but with three men breathing down his neck on 4 apiece, this remains very much a live contest. One careless waft at a wide one and the whole leaderboard could be rewritten. 📅 Looking ahead So the exclusive zero club takes a rare week's holiday — enjoy the peace while it lasts. Next time out, someone somewhere will surely offer themselves up for ceremonial induction. We watch. We wait. We celebrate. 🦆 0️⃣

🦆 Duck Report — 15 to 19 September 2012

6 ducks
Week 19 · 15 Sep 2012 – 19 Sep 2012
Three fixtures, six ducks, and the exclusive zero club welcomes its usual mixed bag of the unlucky and the serially unproductive this week. On with the ceremony. 🏏 Saturday — 1st XI v Sidcup CC (h) Openers beware: Harry Bee fell first, caught Tom Hopkins bowled Christian Stent, for his 2nd duck of the season and 5th of a career now standing 170th of 1407 all-time. A long-standing member of the Debutante Duck and Quack-to-Quack honours boards from his own playing history, Harry now sits level on 5 career ducks with Sam Boyns (2007–2012, 54 innings) — kindred spirits in the art of not scoring. Kevin Presneill followed him back not long after, caught Henry Walker bowled Tom Hopkins, his 1st duck of the season but 18th of a career that now ranks 62nd of 1407. He draws level with Russell Baldwin (1988–2006, 208 innings) on that very tally — proof that eighteen noughts is a perfectly respectable body of work in Linton company. 🌱 Sunday — Sunday 1st XI v Cowdrey CC (h) Stuart Cheesman, caught Gary Marshall bowled Alex Jessop, picked up his 1st duck of the season and 41st of his career, now 20th of 1407 all-time — a stalwart of the Full House board (a duck at every position, 1 to 11) and twice Duck of the Year, in 1994 and 1996, for good measure. A chip off the old block too, with father Fred Cheesman sitting on 42 career ducks and brother Steve on 14. Trevor Back wasn't far behind, caught Oscar Holmes bowled Nicholas Farr, his 3rd duck of the season and 75th career duck, keeping him at a lofty 5th of 1407 all-time and squarely inside the Ducks on the Road, Homebody Duck and Lone Duck top tens. Both Cheesman and Back share a parallel: on this very day in 1962, Richard Ashby made a duck against Staplehurst CC on his way to 28 career noughts. 🕯️ Midweek — Midweek XI v Penshurst Park CC (a) Richard Piper, caught Julius Marshal bowled David Smith, chalked up his 2nd duck of the season and 61st of his career, holding at a remarkable 7th of 1407 all-time — Duck of the Year 2003, a Full House member, and a fixture of three separate top-10 honours boards. On this day in 1982, David Iliffe made a duck of his own against Anchorians CC en route to 31 career ducks. Piper's duck-collecting evidently runs in the family too, with father Brian on 23 and brother James on 11. Peter Whyte joined him in ignominy, bowled David Smith, his 1st duck of the season and 23rd career duck, 52nd of 1407 all-time — level on tally with Brian Piper (1973–2008, 441 innings), a tidy coincidence given who was at the other end that day. Whyte's sibling Tom carries 2 career ducks of his own. 📊 The Week in Short Six ducks from three matches, spread evenly across bat and ball 0️⃣ — no golden ducks, no pairs, and no family affairs this time, just honest, hard-earned failure all round. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Will Thew — 30 inns, 6 ducks (20.0%) - Matthew Ward — 8 inns, 4 ducks (50.0%) - Ray Dixon — 11 inns, 4 ducks (36.36%) - Thomas Beeney — 21 inns, 4 ducks (19.05%) - Adam Wright — 7 inns, 3 ducks (42.86%) The season's final weeks beckon — who'll be joining the honours boards next time out? The zero club never sleeps. 🦆🍩

Duck Report — 8-9 September 2012

3 ducks
Week 18 · 8 Sep 2012 – 9 Sep 2012
Three fixtures took the field this weekend and, in a rare show of restraint, only one of them troubled the Duck Club. Step forward the Sunday 1st XI, away at Hollingbourne CC, who did all the heavy lifting with three ducks in a single innings while the other two sides kept their noses embarrassingly clean. 🏏 Sunday 1st XI v Hollingbourne CC Harry Bee got proceedings underway at number 3, run out for nought without troubling the bowlers. It's his 1st duck of the season and 4th of a 30-innings career, sliding him to 194th of 1407 in the all-time roll call — level on career ducks with -Matthew Ward, who reached the same tally in a far more concentrated 10 innings back in 2011-2012. Harry is also a paid-up member of both the Debutante Duck club and the Retirement Duck honours, standing reminders that his very first innings and, presumptively, his very last have both ended on nought. Today, happily for the archives, was neither. Next in was Ray Dixon, bowled for a duck at four — his 4th of the season and 17th of the career, good enough for 67th on the all-time list. That puts him level with Clive Newman, who needed 371 innings across two decades (1992-2012) to reach the same haul — Ray is getting there rather more efficiently. A former Duck of the Year back in 2007, he's proving this season he's far from finished. Then Simon Thirkell, caught behind off Miles Henslow at six, made it three in the innings — his 2nd duck of the season and a hefty 59th of the career, 9th on the all-time leaderboard. Simon holds proud membership of the "50 Before a 50" club, 59 career ducks and a highest score of just 48 to show for it — triumph through adversity indeed. A three-time Duck of the Year (1983, 2001, 2006) and a veteran of that barren Runaway Duck season, his tally of 59 draws him level with none other than his own brother Nigel Thirkell. Quacking, it seems, runs firmly in the family. 📋 Week in Summary Three ducks, three Sunday 1st XI victims, one thoroughly productive Hollingbourne afternoon. The 1st XI top order showed consistency of a very particular kind, while the rest of the club got away scot-free. Can't win them all, or indeed anything, some days. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard - Will Thew — 6 ducks, 29 innings (20.69%) - -Matthew Ward — 4 ducks, 8 innings (50.0%) - Ray Dixon — 4 ducks, 10 innings (40.0%) - Thomas Beeney — 4 ducks, 20 innings (20.0%) - -Adam Wright — 3 ducks, 7 innings (42.86%) Ray Dixon's afternoon at Hollingbourne keeps him firmly wedged in the top 5 — the race for Duck of the Year is very much alive. 🍩 No doubt the next fixture will add a name or two to these lists. Get out there, middle it or don't — either way, the Duck Club is watching. 0️⃣🦆
