🗓️ 24 Sep – 30 Sep 2005 · 2005 — 1 match

Sat 24 Sep 2005 🧢 1st XIv Chelsfield CC · HomeformWDDLW

Saturday 24 September saw us welcome Chelsfield CC, and the coin, as is so often its habit against me, landed the wrong way up — Chelsfield elected to bat, and off we trotted into the field.

They set about building a total with quiet efficiency, closing on 161 for 5 from their 45 overs. X Vaughan was the chief architect with a polished 58, well supported by H Abbett's 32, and between them they threatened to bat us out of the game before tea had even been considered. Credit to the attack, though — Michael Beeney was the pick of the bowlers with 2 for 37, Jon Harris chipped away for 1 for 15, and I managed to prise one out myself for 17 runs. Darren Styles, meanwhile, bowled with the sort of miserly discipline that makes batsmen mutter into their gloves, sending down two maidens for good measure.

A target of 162 is never a formality, but Richard Piper made it look positively leisurely, cruising to an unbeaten 58, with Mark Christmas playing a handsome supporting hand of 45. I came in and added 25 not out, glad to get bat on ball and see us home in good order, seven wickets in hand and overs to spare.

All told, a thoroughly satisfying afternoon's cricket — disciplined bowling, a calm and composed run chase, and the sort of result that makes the long drive to the ground on a Saturday morning entirely worthwhile. A good day for the club, and no arguments from the captain's chair on that score.

Wed 21 Sep 2005 🧢 Midweek XIv P Penshurst · AwayformDDLDD

The road to Penshurst on a fine Wednesday evening, and for once the coin fell my way. I had no hesitation in choosing to bat, and it turned out rather a good idea, if I say so myself.

Exel battled hard for a well-made 50 and Rob Hunter chipped in with 46, but with D. Smith, J. Heaseman and M. Craig Sands and Michael Beeney continue to lead the season's run and wicket charts respectively. A comprehensive 57-run win, two points banked, and a very pleasant drive home.
