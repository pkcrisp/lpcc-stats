🗓️ 24 Sep – 30 Sep 2016 · 2016 — 1 match

Sat 24 Sep 2016 🧢 1st XIv Stowting CC · HomeformWWWWW

Saturday saw Stowting CC roll into Linton Park under warm, cloudy skies, and winning the toss I did what any self-respecting captain does on a decent-looking pitch — I stuck us in to bat, and for once looked faintly clever for it.

Tobey Castle set about proving me right in some style, carrying his bat for an unbeaten 116, while James Piper chipped in a handsome 58 and Jordan Austin added 33 for good measure. The openers gave us the perfect platform with 65 before the first wicket fell, and a magnificent 115-run stand for the fourth wicket did most of the heavy lifting thereafter. Not everything gleamed, mind — Luke Welsman and Martin Harris both departed for ducks, proof that even in a total of 244/6 there's always room for the odd howler.

Defending that lot, we kept Stowting to 209/7, though David Jackson's well-crafted 62 and support from Mike Christie (37) and Troy Harding (35) made them work for their runs, with a stubborn 72-run fourth-wicket stand the main obstacle. James Piper was again the star turn, snaring 2-7, while I managed to sneak among the wickets myself with 1-3, and Jacob Ryan bowled two tidy maidens on his way to 1-10. Fifteen byes, though, made for an uncomfortable afternoon behind the stumps for poor Nat Presneill.

With Piper now top of the season's run charts on 768 and me sitting third in the wickets list with 18, this was a thoroughly deserved 35-run win — two points banked, and a very pleasant train home.

Sun 18 Sep 2016 🧢 Sunday 1st XIv TC Tenterden CC · HomeformLWLWL

It rained runs at Linton Park on Sunday, and for once the man doing the raining wasn't the sky.

Winning the toss on a warm, cloudy afternoon, I did what any captain with half an eye on the tea urn does and stuck us in. It didn't start promisingly — gone for a duck before the board had troubled the scorers — but what followed was the sort of stand you dream about over the winter nets. Will Thew and Richard Piper put on 168 for the second wicket, Thew compiling a magnificent 92 and Piper right behind him with 84, before Luke Welsman came in to finish things off with an unbeaten 31. Jordan Austin's contribution was, in the strictest statistical sense, nothing at all — golden nothing, as they say — but 232 for 3 from our forty overs was a total to make Tenterden's openers sweat before they'd even padded up.

And sweat they did, eventually. Reuben Collingwood (46), Fred Pearman (41) and Tom Richards (37) gave it a fair go, and their opening pair put on 73 before Welsman got among them — four wickets for 30 in a spell of real teeth, ably backed up by Martin Harris (2-35, and a milestone knock past Terry Donovan into 24th on the all-time list) and a tidy Thew (1-9). Poor Nat Presneill will want to forget the eight byes, mind.

198 for 9 wasn't enough. Thirty-four runs the margin, two points the reward, and thoroughly deserved both.

Sat 03 Sep 2016 🧢 2nd XIv FC Fordcombe CC · HomeformWLLWL

This is a lovely one to write — pure club-cricket colour on top of a clean set of facts.

Fordcombe arrived on a Saturday that couldn't decide whether it was proper cricket weather or not — a fine drizzle settling over the ground as we lost the toss and were promptly asked to bat, which on a day like that felt less like a decision and more like a small act of mischief from the opposition captain.

Forty-three runs to the good, two points banked, and — with Piper still leading our run charts and Wright our wickets for the season — a thoroughly deserved afternoon's work, drizzle and all.
