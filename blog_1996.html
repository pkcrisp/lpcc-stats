🦆 Duck News — 28 September 1996

0 ducks
Week 23 · 28 Sep 1996
Season 1996 draws to its final furlong, and Linton Park turned out for one last fixture of the campaign. The Duck Club's scouts were out in force, nets primed, honours board dusted down and ready for a late addition… …and, agonisingly, nothing. Not a sausage. Not a nought. All parties at the crease this week found their way off the mark, leaving the zero column untroubled for the only time it mattered. A "vegan affair" as the old reports call it — no fowl whatsoever. Whether this counts as a triumph of application or simply a quiet conspiracy against the Duck Club's editorial team is, as ever, a matter of perspective. Either way, the art of not scoring took a rare Saturday off. Spare a thought for the season's front-runners below, who will have to make do with their existing tallies as 1996 edges towards the close — no fresh blows landed to disturb the standings this week, for better or worse. 0️⃣ Duck Hunting Season leaderboard — 1996 0️⃣ 🥇 Stuart Cheesman — 8 ducks in 41 innings (19.51%) 🥈 Mike Elworthy — 5 ducks in 31 innings (16.13%) 🥉 Peter Whyte — 4 ducks in 11 innings (36.36% — a quite alarming strike rate) 4. Richard Ellis — 4 ducks in 13 innings (30.77%) 5. Roly Edwards — 4 ducks in 17 innings (23.53%) Peter Whyte remains the statistical menace of the pack — better than one duck in every three trips to the crease is the kind of consistency the club can only doff its cap to, in the wrong direction. With the season winding down, there's precious little time left for anyone to mount a late charge on Stuart Cheesman's commanding lead at the top. But this is the Duck Club — complacency has undone finer averages than these. One careless waft, one full toss straight into the pads, and the standings could yet be rewritten before the covers go on for winter. 🏏 Until the next fixture, when normal service — and the odd golden duck — will surely resume. Keep your bats straight, or better yet, don't. 🍩

🦆 Duck News — 21–25 September 1996 🦆

2 ducks
Week 22 · 21 Sep 1996 – 25 Sep 1996
A quieter week for the Club by our lofty standards — just two ducks laid down all week, both in the very same innings, both for the 1st XI. Quality over quantity, evidently. 🏏 1st XI v Hastings & St Leonards Priory CC (home, 21 Sept) Keith Hatcher went in at first drop and departed bowled A Barnes for nought — his 2nd duck of the season and 7th of an increasingly decorated career, now sat 108th of 1219 in the all-time roll of dishonour. A neat bit of calendar symmetry here too: on this very date back in 1958, Stan Wratten (1943–1984, 35 career ducks, all-time 17th) was also trudging back for a duck, against Tilling Steven. History, it seems, enjoys an anniversary. Four down the order, Stuart Cheesman edged Carl Read for his 8th duck of the summer — comfortably the Club's most prolific quacker this year — and his 22nd career duck from 108 innings, 42nd of 1219 all-time. Stuart is a paid-up member of the Debutante Duck club, having ducked on his very first senior innings, and already crowned Duck of the Year 1996 to go with his earlier honours in 1994 — the leaderboard below shows exactly why. He's also a Full House member, having now recorded a duck in every position from 1 to 11, dedication bordering on the pathological. The ducking gene clearly runs in the family too — brother Steve carries 13 career ducks and father Fred a hearty 29. And like Keith, Stuart's own fall echoes Stan Wratten's duck on this exact date in 1958. 📅 Week in Summary Four fixtures played, only one of them troubled by the zero — a rare outbreak of competence from the other three sides. The 1st XI alone kept the Club's reputation intact, striking twice in one innings against Hastings & St Leonards. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard — top 5 - Stuart Cheesman — 40 innings, 8 ducks (20.0%) - Mike Elworthy — 30 innings, 5 ducks (16.67%) - Peter Whyte — 11 innings, 4 ducks (36.36%) - Richard Ellis — 13 innings, 4 ducks (30.77%) - Roly Edwards — 16 innings, 4 ducks (25.0%) Stuart sits well clear at the summit as the season draws on — can anyone catch him before the books close on 1996? Get out there next fixture and give the Club something fresh to celebrate. 🦆🍩0️⃣

🦆 Duck News — 14–15 September 1996

7 ducks
Week 21 · 14 Sep 1996 – 15 Sep 1996
Three fixtures, seven fresh entries in the ledger. The 1996 zero club had a busy weekend. 🏏 Saturday 14th — 1st XI at Clarion CC A torrid afternoon for Linton's middle and lower order, with four names added inside one innings. Stuart Cheesman went first, lbw to Steve Harris for his 7th duck of the season and 21st of a career now 105 innings deep — 43rd on the all-time list of 1218. Stuart already wears the Duck of the Year crown for both 1994 and 1996, and carries the rare Full House honour (a duck at every position, 1 through 11) besides. Clearly a family trade too — father Fred sits on 29 career ducks, brother Steve on 13. Richard Ellis (b E Driver) followed for his 3rd of the season, 7th of his career — a long-standing Debutante Duck club member from his very first Linton innings years back. He wasn't done for the weekend, either. Matt Large edged behind off E Driver for his 3rd of the season, but the career column is the real headline: 53 ducks, 5th of all 1218 names ever to bat for the club, with six Duck of the Year titles (1987, '88, '89, '92, '93, '95) gathering dust on the mantelpiece. Adam Pooley completed the quartet lbw to E Driver, his 3rd of the season and 6th career duck. All four kept fine historical company — on this very day in 1958, Stan Wratten also trudged off for nought, against Tunbridge Wells CC, one of 35 career ducks that still rank him 17th of all time. 🏏 Saturday 14th — 2nd XI v Betsham CC Iain Cardew edged Simon Rasho through to the keeper for his 1st duck of the season, 3rd of his career, and — like Richard Ellis before him — a Debutante Duck man from way back. Same 1958 vintage in the history books as his Clarion teammates above. 🏏 Sunday 15th — 1st XI v Northend Sunday Peter Whyte fell lbw to K Stapley for his 4th of the season, 16th of his career — 54th all-time. Kin note: brother Tom carries 2 career ducks of his own. And Richard Ellis was back at it, caught behind off J-Robertson for a second duck in as many days — his 4th of the season, 8th of his career, and a proper pair to bookend the weekend across Saturday and Sunday. Both Sunday casualties shared company with 1982, when Trevor Hales made his own duck against The Mote CC, one of just 3 in a career running 1981 to 1989. 📊 Week in Summary Seven ducks from three matches, four of them from a single Clarion innings, and E Driver walking off with three scalps to his name. Richard Ellis the week's stand-out repeat offender with a duck on both days. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard 1. Stuart Cheesman — 37 inns, 7 ducks (18.92%) 2. Mike Elworthy — 28 inns, 5 ducks (17.86%) 3. Peter Whyte — 9 inns, 4 ducks (44.44%) 4. Richard Ellis — 13 inns, 4 ducks (30.77%) 5. Roly Edwards — 16 inns, 4 ducks (25.0%) Stuart holds the lead but Peter Whyte's strike rate is the one to fear. The ducks never rest — see you at the next fixture. 🦆

Week 20 · 7 Sep 1996 – 11 Sep 1996
A quiet week for the exclusive zero club — four matches in the diary and the ducks mostly kept their heads down. Only one soul was brave (or careless) enough to add his name to the ledger. Midweek XI Lord Cornwallis XI provided the solitary casualty, with Mike Elworthy caught J Cornwallis, bowled S Ives at number four. He survived at least a ball before departing, so no golden duck ignominy to report — just the plain, honest business of getting out for nought. It's his 5th of the season and a tidy 22nd for his Linton career, from 227 innings, lodging him at number 41 of 1216 in the all-time table — a spot in rarefied company, and a name that already appears in the Tourist Duck top 10, so he's clearly been collecting these on the road as well as at home. Fittingly, on this very day back in 1991, Fred Cheesman was doing exactly the same thing against The Mote CC, en route to his own 29 career ducks and a place at number 26 all-time — a two-time Duck of the Year and proud member of the Full House club (a duck at every position, no less). History, as ever, repeats itself in the most scoreless of ways. Week in Summary One duck from four matches is about as restrained as this club gets — either the bowlers were off form or, more likely, everyone else simply couldn't be bothered to get out. Consistency of a very particular kind from Mike, carrying the flag alone this week. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 🥇 1. Stuart Cheesman — 6 ducks, 35 innings (17.14%) 2. 🦆 Duck News — Weekend of 31 August to 1 September 1996

1 duck
Week 19 · 31 Aug 1996 – 1 Sep 1996
Saturday 31st August 2nd XI away at Sibton Park CC and it's the exclusive zero club's only guest of the entire weekend. Paul Bowles, promoted to number 5, was bowled by P- Mc Leary without so much as troubling the scorers. It's his 2nd duck of the 1996 campaign and the 52nd of a career that now stretches to 858 innings, keeping him lodged at 6th in the all-time standings out of 1,216 names on the books. A club stalwart of the very particular kind of consistency the Duck Club exists to honour — Bowles is already a two-time Duck of the Year (1966 and 1968), sits in the Ducks on the Road top 10 for his willingness to get out for nought in unfamiliar postcodes, and shares the Only Two Quacks honour with Adam Pooley. Sibton Park, it seems, agreed with the bowlers rather more than the batting order. Neat historical footnote for the anoraks among us — on this very day back in 1980, Allan Cooper also fell for a duck against Staplehurst CC. Cooper played from 1971 to 1989 and retired with 38 to his name, good for 13th all-time, and picked up two Only Two Quacks honours of his own along the way (with Jim Hall and with Jon Harris) plus a third with Trevor Back. Sixteen years apart, same fate, same calendar square. Sunday 1st September Elsewhere across the club a rare and welcome vegan weekend — the second fixture came and went without a single nought to report. The ducks, evidently, took the Sunday off. Week in Summary A quiet one by Duck Club standards — just the single addition across two matches, all the damage confined to Sibton Park. Sometimes the art of not scoring takes a rest day too, though with Bowles still active and hovering at 52, nobody expects the silence to last. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Stuart Cheesman — 6 ducks in 32 innings (18.75%) - Trevor Back — 4 ducks in 16 innings (25.00%) - Rob Doick — 4 ducks in 19 innings (21.05%) - Roly Edwards — 4 ducks in 13 innings (30.77%) - Mike Elworthy — 4 ducks in 25 innings (16.00%) Edwards holds the grim honour of the sharpest strike rate up top, but with Cheesman's innings count climbing fastest, this race for Duck of the Year has plenty of overs left in it yet. Next fixture awaits, and with it the next round of nominations for the honours board. Pads on, bails off — see you all out there. 🦆🍩

🦆 Duck News — Week of 24 August 1996

7 ducks
Week 18 · 24 Aug 1996 – 30 Aug 1996
Seven ducks hatched across five matches this week, one of them the purest kind there is. Read on. 1st XI Bearsted CC, 26 August: last man in, Ollie Priestly departed first ball, bowled by Sarath Chandrasekera — a golden duck, the ignominy no one asked for. His second of the season lifts him to 5 career ducks, 138th of 1216 all-time, a paid-up member of the Debutante Duck club from a debut long past. On this very day back in 1979, Tim Thirkell was performing the identical trick against Old Anchorians CC. 2nd XI Away at Marden, four wickets fell for nought in one grim afternoon. Stewart Morrison opened and went lbw for his 3rd of the season, moving to 18 career ducks, 49th all-time — a Full House nearly-man still one position shy of the complete set, and only a handful ahead of sibling Phillip Morrison's own tally. Trevor Back followed at first drop, caught off the Milstead double act, his 4th of the season and 46th of a storied career that sits him 10th on the all-time list, a fixture of the Lone Duck and Homebody Duck top tens. Stuart Cheesman continued the procession at number 7, his 6th of the season taking him to 20 career ducks — and with "Duck of the Year 1994" and, tellingly, "Duck of the Year 1996" already on his board, he's the man everyone else is chasing this season. A chip off a very ducky block too, with parent Fred Cheesman on 29 career noughts and brother Steve on 13. Chris Tymms closed the innings at 8 with his maiden duck of the season, his 7th in a 39-innings career. Remarkably, the whole middle order was in the company of Benny Bell, who made his own duck against Aylesford Paper Mills CC on this exact date back in 1968 — Marden clearly has a taste for anniversaries. Tour XI Daniel Thirkell endured a tour to forget, registering a pair — bowled for nought against Mullion on the 28th, then caught and bowled by Seth Turner against Falmouth CC the very next day. That's career ducks 4 and 5 from 31 innings, 129th all-time both times. Fitting company for a man born into Duck Club royalty — the Thirkell name already carries well over 200 career ducks between John, Tim, Nigel, Simon and Stuart. His first echoed Piers McDonald's 1993 duck against Sheldwich CC, his second Stan Wratten's 1954 effort against D Hambrook XI. 📊 Week in summary: 7 ducks from 5 matches, one golden duck at Bearsted, a grim pair on tour, and Marden proving fatal for the entire 2nd XI middle order. No family ducks to report this week — the bloodlines behaved themselves, for once. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 1. Stuart Cheesman — 6 ducks, 32 innings (18.75%) 2. Roly Edwards — 4 ducks, 12 innings (33.33%) 3. Trevor Back — 4 ducks, 16 innings (25%) 4. Rob Doick — 4 ducks, 18 innings (22.22%) 5. Mike Elworthy — 4 ducks, 24 innings (16.67%) Cheesman leads the count, Edwards leads the strike rate — the race for Duck of the Year 1996 is well and truly on. Next fixture awaits, bat in hand and destiny uncertain. See you all out there. 🦆🍩0️⃣
