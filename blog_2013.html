🦆 Duck News — Sunday 22 September 2013 🦆

1 duck
Week 23 · 22 Sep 2013
A quiet weekend on the Linton duck-hunting front — just the one fixture, just the one nought — but never let it be said the Club doesn't do quality over quantity. Sunday 1st XI v Tenterden CC Mark Christmas strode in at four and departed for a golden-duck-adjacent 2 balls faced, bowled by Tom Richards before he'd troubled the scorers. No first-baller ignominy here, mind — he at least survived long enough to inspect the surroundings before the timbers went. This is Mark's 4th duck of the season and his 26th for the club in 302 innings, easing him to 42nd of 1,415 in the all-time reckoning — rarefied, if slightly damp, company. He does so as a paid-up name on the Duck of the Year 2008 roll of honour, and as a permanent, tantalising near-miss on the Full House board, having ducked in 10 of the 11 possible positions — one solitary slot still eluding him. The suspense, frankly, is unbearable. Fittingly, Mark's 26 career ducks draw him level with club stalwart Paul Brattle, who plied his trade from 1969 to 2008 and reached the same tally in 308 innings — sitting one place below him at 43rd all-time, and himself a Debutante Duck club member from an era when Linton Park's outfield was presumably a great deal muddier. Week in Summary One match, one duck — the Duck Club equivalent of a training run rather than a full hunt. Still, every nought counts toward the greater ledger, and Mark's contribution keeps the autumn embers of the season glowing nicely. 🍩 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard 🍩 1. Nat Presneill — 7 ducks / 17 innings (41.18%) 2. Will Thirkell — 6 ducks / 44 innings (13.64%) 3. Matthew Carter — 5 ducks / 30 innings (16.67%) 4. Craig Sands — 5 ducks / 36 innings (13.89%) 5. Will Thew — 5 ducks / 38 innings (13.16%) Nat Presneill remains imperious atop the table with a duck rate any bowler would envy, but with the season drawing on there's still time for a late surge from the chasing pack. As ever, the Club watches, waits, and quietly hopes the next fixture delivers a fresh crop of noughts for the annals. 🦆🏏0️⃣

Three matches in the diary this weekend but the harvest was concentrated entirely on the road, where Linton's Sunday 1st XI travelled to Mersham Sports CC and generously donated three noughts to the cause. Elsewhere, the other two fixtures passed off as strictly vegan affairs — not a feather ruffled. 🏏 Sunday 1st XI at Mersham Sports CC Thomas Beeney got proceedings underway at number 2, managing nine balls of resistance before being bowled by George Hansen. It's Ray's 3rd of the season and 20th of his career, moving him to 61st all-time. A former Duck of the Year back in 2007, Ray shows the old instincts haven't left him. He now draws level on 20 career ducks with Dave Todd, who took a rather more leisurely 233 innings across 1978–2013 to get there — Ray's version came at considerably greater pace. Rounding things off at number 10, Will Bramley-Harker fell to Jack Wilson after six balls, his 2nd duck of the season and 4th of a fledgling 17-innings career, placing him 215th on the all-time roll. Will joins Matthew Ward, who reached the same career total of 4 in just 10 innings across 2011–2012, so there's still time to close that gap either way. Will is also a long-standing name on the Retirement Duck honours board from a past farewell — a reminder the club never forgets a good exit, even one from years back. And keeping it in the family, brother Matt Bramley-Harker already carries 2 career ducks of his own — the surname is clearly not scoring runs this decade. 📊 Week in Summary Three ducks, one unlucky top order, and Mersham Sports CC doing rather too good a job with the ball.
