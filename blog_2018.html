🦆 Duck News — 22 September 2018 🦆

0 ducks
Week 23 · 22 Sep 2018
A single fixture out on the green this week, and — for once — the quacking classes were nowhere to be found. Every bat that walked out, walked back with at least a run to its name. No ducks recorded. No golden ducks, no pairs, not so much as a nervous single-ball dismissal to report. Rare, and frankly a little unsettling. The Duck Club exists to celebrate the art of not scoring, and this week the membership let the side down by scoring instead. One suspects a few of the regulars had a quiet word with the umpires beforehand. Still, the season standings remain fiercely contested, and the leaderboard waits for no man's clean sheet: Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 🏆🦆 1. John Karassellos — 8 ducks from 17 innings (47.06%) — the runaway leader, closing in on being dismissed for nought roughly every other visit to the crease. A truly committed campaign. 2. Ian Hollingworth — 5 ducks from 8 innings (62.5%) — the division's most efficient collector, ducking at a rate the rest of the club can only admire from a safe distance. 3. Thuraiappah Pushpavinothan — 5 ducks from 20 innings (25.0%) — steady, patient accumulation of noughts, one in every four trips. 4. Benjamin Austin — 5 ducks from 21 innings (23.81%) — level on ducks with the top three but working harder for each one, bless him. 5. Will Thew — 4 ducks from 16 innings (25.0%) — rounding out the top five and keeping the chasing pack honest. Karassellos remains the clear favourite for Duck of the Year with the campaign well into its back half, but Hollingworth's strike rate is the one to watch — at that pace he could yet close the gap on volume alone. A quiet week for the honours board, then, but the season is far from over. 🍩 With fixtures still to come, normal service — dismissal for nought, in all its ignominious glory — is expected to resume shortly. Get out there, middle nothing, and give the correspondent something to write about next week. 0️⃣🏏

🦆 Duck News — 16 September 2018

0 ducks
Week 22 · 16 Sep 2018
A single fixture on the books this week, and for once the Duck Club must report... nothing. Not a solitary nought. Eleven Linton batsmen took guard and, in an act of collective defiance the club rarely witnesses, every single one of them troubled the scorer. The zero club sits empty this week — doors locked, kettle off, honours board gathering dust. It happens. Even the most dedicated hunting season needs the occasional close season of its own, and this was it: one match, no ducks, no drama. The art of not scoring took a rare week off, rather ironically by everyone succeeding at scoring instead. Do not mistake this for a crisis of form, however. The season standings remain fiercely contested below, and with the campaign still very much alive there's ample time for the drought to break spectacularly next weekend. Ducks, like poultry generally, have a habit of turning up when you least expect them. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 🥇🍩 1. John Karassellos — 8 ducks / 17 innings (47.1%) 2. Ian Hollingworth — 5 ducks / 8 innings (62.5%) 0️⃣ 3. Thuraiappah Pushpavinothan — 5 ducks / 20 innings (25.0%) 4. Benjamin Austin — 5 ducks / 21 innings (23.8%) 5. Will Thew — 4 ducks / 15 innings (26.7%) Ian Hollingworth continues to lead the percentages with a frankly alarming conversion rate — over six in ten trips to the crease ending in nought is the kind of consistency the club exists to celebrate. John Karassellos meanwhile holds top spot on volume, eight ducks deep into a season that shows no sign of mercy. Spare a thought, too, for those lurking just off this table — the hunting season is long, and a quiet week like this one has a habit of luring the overconfident back to the crease. Roll on the next fixture, when normal service — and no doubt a fresh crop of golden ducks, pairs, and the odd king pair — will surely resume. The zero club never stays closed for long. 🏏🦆

🦆 Duck Report — 8 to 12 September 2018 🦆

7 ducks
Week 21 · 8 Sep 2018 – 12 Sep 2018
Three fixtures, seven ducks, and a midweek match at Penshurst that did more damage than the entire weekend put together. The exclusive zero club welcomes its members back to the fold. Saturday 8th — 1st XI v Limpsfield Chart CC (h) Openers of ignominy Thuraiappah Pushpavinothan and Charles Curteis both fell to the same Charles House-Martin Phillips combination in short order. Pushpavinothan chalks up his 5th of the season and 6th career duck from 33 innings, sitting 176th of 1479 all time — and, rather neatly, on this day in 1981 Richard Ashby (1962–1995, 28 ducks, a Tourist Duck top-10 man) also fell against Paul CC. Curteis, meanwhile, notches his 2nd of the season and 6th career, and finds the exact same historical company on the exact same date. John Karassellos went next, caught behind for his 7th season duck and 11th career, level with Andrew Moss (1968–2008, 135 innings) on the all-time list. As reigning Duck of the Year 2018 and a proud member of the Ducks in a Row club (5 consecutive — a genuinely fearsome run), Karassellos is clearly not done yet. Martin Harris rounded things off, chopping on for his 3rd of the season and 28th career duck, drawing level with Christopher Thirkell (1998–2015, 157 innings). A Duck of the Year 2011 alumnus, and a chip off no ordinary block — his son Jon Harris carries 93 career ducks of his own. Sunday 9th — Sunday 1st XI v Cobham CC, Kent (h) Trevor Back added to his imperious collection, his 3rd duck of the season and a staggering 87th of his career from 761 innings — good enough for 4th on the all-time leaderboard, with Ducks on the Road, Homebody Duck and Lone Duck top-10 memberships to his name, plus shared quacking honours with Allan Cooper and Jon Harris. On this very day in 2000, Daniel Thirkell (1993–2018, 30 ducks) also made the walk, against Bearsted CC. Wednesday 12th — Midweek XI v Penshurst Park CC (a) Jake Cheesman ran through the middle order, and two names from Saturday came straight back for more. Martin Harris departed first ball — a genuine golden duck — for his 4th of the season and 29th of his career, completing a grim weekly pair after Saturday's effort. On this day in 1959, S Lusher suffered the same fate against Bowaters CC. John Karassellos followed for his 8th season duck and 12th career, also completing his own pair for the week, and joins a splendidly crowded shelf on 12 — including Bert Buckoke, who reached the same mark across 1928–1953 and twice took Duck of the Year honours along the way. Week in summary Seven ducks from three matches, with Penshurst Park doing the week's heaviest lifting. Two full pairs recorded — Harris and Karassellos both fell twice — and Karassellos continues to build a formidable case for retaining his Duck of the Year 2018 crown. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - John Karassellos — 17 inns, 8 ducks (47.06%) - Ian Hollingworth — 8 inns, 5 ducks (62.5%) - Thuraiappah Pushpavinothan — 20 inns, 5 ducks (25.0%) - Benjamin Austin — 21 inns, 5 ducks (23.81%) - Will Thew — 15 inns, 4 ducks (26.67%) Next fixture beckons, and the zero club never sleeps 🦆🏏 — bring your bats, gentlemen, or at least a straight face for the walk back.

🦆 Duck Report — 1-2 September 2018

3 ducks
Week 20 · 1 Sep 2018 – 2 Sep 2018
September arrived and, true to form, so did the ducks. Four matches, three fresh entries to the exclusive zero club — let's get into it. 🏏 Saturday 1st — 1st XI v Speldhurst CC (h) Daniel Thirkell fell early at first wicket down, bowled by Harry Fitzgerald, for his opening duck of the season and the 30th of a mighty 492-innings career — 35th on the all-time list of 1,479. He lands on exactly the tally held by Luke Welsman (2012–2018), who reached his 30 in a far leaner 162 innings and carries three Duck of the Year titles (2015-17) plus a near-miss on the Full House board for his trouble. Daniel also sits proudly, and unsurprisingly, inside cricket's most committed duck-laying bloodline — kin Tim Thirkell (94), John Thirkell (90), Nigel Thirkell (60) and Simon Thirkell (59) between them accounting for a small nation's worth of noughts. Some families collect stamps. 🏏 Saturday 1st — 2nd XI v West Farleigh CC (a) At number seven, Brian Piper was bowled by Tom Osborne for his first duck of the season and 24th of the career — 445 innings gone, 58th all-time. He draws level with Nat Presneill (2010–2018), whose 24 came in a brisker 94 innings and who holds the rare Quack-to-Quack double, duck on debut and duck on farewell, capped by a Retirement Duck credit. Piper carries the family torch too, with parent-child kin Richard Piper (82, one of the club's true stalwarts of failure) and James Piper (18), plus sibling David Piper (7) also on the books. 🍩 Sunday 2nd — Sunday 2nd XI v Chestfield CC (h) The weekend's showpiece. John Karassellos, batting eight, was bowled by Robson Hulks first ball — a fully certified golden duck, gone before his eyes adjusted to the light. It's his sixth of the season, enough to lead the entire Duck Hunting table outright, and his tenth for the career from just 47 innings, 122nd all-time. The honours board already has him down as reigning Duck of the Year 2018, mid-way through a run of Ducks in a Row (five consecutive) and edging towards Full House nearly-man status with 10 of 11 positions covered. He joins Jack Jenner (2003–2008) on ten career ducks, Jenner having got there in 53 innings before signing off with a Retirement Duck of his own. The Karassellos name runs deep too — kin Anthony Karassellos already has a solitary career duck to his name. 📋 Week in Summary Three ducks from four matches — modest by the club's own extravagant standards, but quality over quantity: one golden, one dynasty double-header, and a season leader extending his grip on top spot. No pairs, no king pairs, and the family ducks column stayed refreshingly, disappointingly empty this week. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 1. John Karassellos — 6 ducks / 15 inns (40.0%) 2. Ian Hollingworth — 5 ducks / 8 inns (62.5%) 3. Benjamin Austin — 5 ducks / 20 inns (25.0%) 4. Will Thew — 4 ducks / 15 inns (26.67%) 5. Thuraiappah Pushpavinothan — 4 ducks / 19 inns (21.05%) Karassellos leads on volume, Hollingworth leads on sheer percentage terror. Plenty of season left for the chasing pack to make their move. 🦆 See you all next weekend — the ducks never take a Sunday off, and neither should you. Bat straight, watch the ball, and if all else fails, at least fall for a memorable one.

Eight ducks banked in total — a fine haul of triumph-through-adversity for the archives. 🏏 Saturday, 1st XI away at Penshurst Park CC Warren Jackson got things moving early, trapped lbw by David Smith for his maiden LPCC duck — 1 for the season, 1 for the career, in 12 innings so far. Rank 705 of 1478 and climbing the wrong way already. On this very day back in 1980, Martin Thompson made a duck against West Farleigh CC on his way to 10 career noughts across a 1975-1987 stint that ended, fittingly, with a Retirement Duck to his name. 🏏 Saturday, 2nd XI at home to St Lawrence CC A proper family occasion here. Thuraiappah Pushpavinothan edged behind for his 3rd of the season and 4th career duck in 27 innings, drawing level with -Matthew Ward's tally from 2011-12.
