Wed 19 Aug 2026 🧢 Midweek XIv Tunbridge Wells Wednesday CC · HomeformWWWLL

Wednesday the 19th of August brought Tunbridge Wells Wednesday CC to Linton Park under a warm, damp sky that promised more discomfort for fielders than batters. I lost the toss and we were asked to field first, which on a sticky afternoon is rarely the invitation you'd choose, but the lads set about it with good cheer regardless.

When our turn came to bat, we posted a healthy 194 for 5 from 34 overs. Benjamin Austin was the architect, batting through for an unbeaten 85 that anchored the innings from start to finish, with Aaron Hughes 33 and Luke Welsman 28 adding welcome heft either side of him.

Defending that total asked plenty of our bowling attack, and the visitors dug in gamely, closing their innings unbeaten as J Harcourt made a composed 57 not out and J Carthew chipped in with a well-crafted fifty of his own. Zach Wilde was pick of the bowlers with 2-23, well supported by Nolan Wilde's 2-27 and Thomas Beeney's 1-22, but Tunbridge Wells held firm to the close.

There was a personal landmark tucked into the afternoon too: Benjamin Austin's 85 eased him past Kevin Presneill and into 23rd on the club's all-time run-scorers list, while his 251 runs keep him top of our 2026 Midweek XI run charts, with Thomas Beeney leading the wickets column on nine.

A draw, then — honest toil from both sides on a pitch that gave neither the edge. Honours shared, and on we go.
