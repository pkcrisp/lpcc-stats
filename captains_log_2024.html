Sat 21 Sep 2024 🧢 1st XIv Sissinghurst CC · AwayformDDDDD

A warm, damp Saturday saw us travel to Sissinghurst, and I'm delighted to report the lads made light work of the conditions. Batting first, we posted a handsome 202/2 from our 35 overs, built on the sort of top-order platform captains dream about and rarely get. Jordan Austin led from the front with a well-made 53 not out, Toby Joseph matched him stroke for stroke with a fine unbeaten fifty of his own, and Luke Welsman chipped in an unbroken 38 to round things off nicely. Even the extras column pulled its weight with 28 — not quite man of the match, but not far off some of our top order's fielding either.

Defending 202 is never a formality, and Sissinghurst gave it a proper go, reaching 143/3 off their 35. Ben Parsons played beautifully for his 74 not out, well supported by Philip Buckingham's 30, and for a spell it looked like we might be made to sweat. But the bowling held firm: Alex Wade was pick of the attack with 1-11, Martin Harris chipped in with 1-29, and Farhan Malik was miserly throughout, sending down four maidens that strangled any momentum they threatened to build. I was glad to get among the action myself with bat in hand earlier in the day, though it was very much a team effort that saw the job done.

A word before I close: Luke Welsman brought up 6,000 career runs for the club, a landmark that deserves far more fanfare than a damp Saturday in Kent could offer. Between that milestone and a comfortable 59-run victory, this was a Saturday to file firmly under "savour."
