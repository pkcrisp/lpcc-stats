🦆 Duck Report — 20-21 September 2025

4 ducks
Week 36 · 20 Sep 2025 – 21 Sep 2025
Five fixtures, four ducks, and one properly historic afternoon at Cudham for a certain father-and-son combination. Let's get into it. 🏏 2nd XI — away to Town Malling CC William Gray dug in for nine deliveries before being bowled by Daniel Clark, falling at first drop for his third duck of the season and fifth of his LPCC career. That tally puts him level with David T Lowen, who played 1970-2023 across 75 innings for the same five — William's getting there rather more efficiently. Little wonder the ducking gene runs deep: brother James Gray already sits on 7 career noughts of his own. Trevor Back followed, caught Kemsley bowled Tunnicliff after a watchful 30 balls. Just his first duck of this campaign, but a staggering 93rd for the club, keeping him lodged at #4 on the all-time list — a fixture of the Ducks on the Road, Homebody Duck and Lone Duck top tens, and a proud co-owner of Quacking Together (with Jon Harris) and Only Two Quacks (with Allan Cooper). Spookily, on this very day back in 2008 Matthew Carter also made a duck, against Sidcup CC. 🍩 Friendly XI — home to Cudham Wyse CC Now for the main event. In walked Stuart Beardwell at 8, first ball, bowled Moazzam Sultan — a golden duck, and on his actual senior debut too, a genuine Debutante Duck the moment the bails went. One place later, in walked his father Euan Beardwell at 9... and departed the very same way, first ball, same bowler, same golden ignominy. A proper Duck and Ducklings double — parent and child both back-to-back golden ducks off Moazzam Sultan, the rarest ceremony the club has to offer, and one for the honours board in its own right. Stuart's is his maiden club duck, joining fellow one-and-doner "- Howard" (2006) on a tally of exactly 1. Euan meanwhile picks up his second of the season and third for his career from 19 innings, drawing level with 2025 new boy Alexander Daniels on 3. 📋 The week in brief Four ducks from five matches, two of them golden and both belonging to the same household — Linton Park truly is a family club. Elsewhere the veterans quietly extended their records: Trevor Back's 93rd remains a monument nobody is threatening any time soon. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - John Karassellos — 25 inns, 7 ducks (28.0%) - Tobey Castle — 25 inns, 6 ducks (24.0%) - Praveen Tunga — 11 inns, 5 ducks (45.45%) - Toby Joseph — 27 inns, 5 ducks (18.52%) - Richard Piper — 28 inns, 5 ducks (17.86%) Praveen's percentage remains genuinely alarming. Next weekend brings fresh opportunity for glory-through-failure — who fancies joining the Beardwells in the family album? 🦆0️⃣
