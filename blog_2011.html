🦆 Duck News — 24-25 September 2011

4 ducks
Week 24 · 24 Sep 2011 – 25 Sep 2011
Saturday 24th September 1st XI v Offham CC — Nathan Brooker took it upon himself to personally escort three Linton batsmen back to the pavilion, all trapped the same way. A wholesale delivery of noughts. - Will Thirkell, in at three, lasted precisely one ball before being bowled by Brooker — a proper golden duck. His 2nd of the season and 9th of a career now standing at 82 innings, keeping him at 117th of 1399 all time, level with names like Gerald Wright and John Bloomfield on the same tally. On this very day in 2003, Brian Piper (1973–2008, 23 ducks, 48th all time) was doing precisely the same thing against Penshurst Park CC. A proud Thirkell duck dynasty too, with the surname littered through the honours board from top to bottom. - Jack Gwynn held out three balls before joining the queue — his first LPCC duck in just 10 innings. He sits level on a single career duck with 2006 one-and-done man - Howard, whose entire duck career began and ended with that same lone nought. - Martin Harris completed Brooker's hat-trick of dismissals, golden duck and all, for his 6th of the season — fitting, given he's already this year's reigning Duck of the Year. That's 8 career ducks from just 28 innings, 126th all time, level with Carl Davis (1994–2009, 41 innings), who also bowed out on a duck. A family trade too — his kin Jon Harris carries a hefty 90 career noughts. Sunday 25th September 1st XI v Chairman's XI — - Thomas Beeney fell to Tom Davey at number nine for his 6th duck of the season, drawing level with Martin Harris at the top of this year's charts. His 19th career duck from 97 innings keeps him a healthy 59th all time, and he remains a paid-up member of the Debutante Duck club and the Ducks on the Road top 10 from seasons past. On this same date in 1960, Len Conway — also a Debutante Duck alumnus — made a duck against Linton Park CC. Thomas's kin Michael Beeney adds 34 further career ducks to the family collection. Week in Summary Four ducks from two matches, the lot of them 1st XI, and three of Saturday's victims falling to the very same bowler — Nathan Brooker will be dining out on that story for a while. A weekend of consistency of a very particular kind. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard 1. Martin Harris — 15 inns, 6 ducks (40.0%) 2. Thomas Beeney — 18 inns, 6 ducks (33.3%) 3. Richard Piper — 32 inns, 5 ducks (15.6%) 4. Richard Knowler — 15 inns, 4 ducks (26.7%) 5. -Adam Wright — 18 inns, 3 ducks (16.7%) Harris and Beeney locked together at the summit — the race for Duck of the Year 2011 is very much alive. 🍩 Next fixture looms, and Brooker's still bowling somewhere. Who's next to join the exclusive zero club? 🦆0️⃣

🦆 Duck News — 10th to 14th September 2011

3 ducks
Week 22 · 10 Sep 2011 – 14 Sep 2011
A slim week for the collectors among us — just three ducks scattered across four fixtures — but each one arrived with a tale worth the telling. Saturday 10th September 2nd XI at Headcorn CC Trevor Back was bowled by James Hopkins at number five, his second duck of the season and the 72nd of a career that shows no sign of slowing. That tally keeps him a lofty 5th in the all-time standings, a paid-up member of the Ducks on the Road, Homebody Duck and Lone Duck top tens, with shared quacking honours alongside Allan Cooper and Jon Harris besides. On this very date back in 1955, John --'s Taylor also made a duck against Old Roffensians — some things, it seems, are written into the fixture list itself. Sunday 11th September Sunday 1st XI v Harbledown CC Martin Harris departed bowled by Neil Harrison at number eight, his fifth duck of the season and a modest seventh for his career from just 26 innings — a strike rate that leaves him out in front, alone, for Duck of the Year 2011. He draws level on career ducks with Jamie Ratcliffe, the club's own Duck of the Year for 2009 — good company keeps finding itself in bad company. Harris also carries the pedigree for it, with kinsman Jon Harris sitting on 90 career ducks of his own. Wednesday 14th September Midweek XI v Penshurst Park CC Michael Beeney was bowled by Paul Eames at number six for his second duck of the season and 34th of his career, holding steady at 27th on the all-time list. Michael is a fully paid-up member of several exclusive societies — the Diamond Duck class of 2002, Retirement Duck honours from a past farewell, and a standing Only Two Quacks partnership with Ian Brattle. On this same date in 1958, Stan Wratten made a duck against Tunbridge Wells CC, later immortalised in his own Quacking Together pairing with John Ames. Week in summary Three ducks from four matches — a quiet week by Linton's usual standards, quality over quantity. All three fell to the bowlers rather than any run-out mishaps, and two of the three duckers are already deep into season tallies that suggest 2011 has plenty more nought to give. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Martin Harris — 5 ducks in 13 innings (38.46%) - Thomas Beeney — 5 ducks in 16 innings (31.25%) - Richard Piper — 5 ducks in 31 innings (16.13%) - Richard Knowler — 4 ducks in 14 innings (28.57%) - -Adam Wright — 3 ducks in 18 innings (16.67%) Martin Harris leads the race for Duck of the Year 2011, with Thomas Beeney lurking just a duck behind and the season not yet done. Next fixture, the hunt resumes — bring your bats, gentlemen, or don't bother bringing them at all. 🦆

🦆 Duck Club Bulletin — 3–4 September 2011 🦆

1 duck
Week 21 · 3 Sep 2011 – 4 Sep 2011
A quiet weekend on the poultry front, but the exclusive zero club never fully closes its doors, and one member duly reported for duty. Saturday 3rd September 2nd XI v Selsdon CC (home) Laurie Probert, walking in at four, was caught behind by Jack Dinning off the bowling of Mike Parry — a tidy, professional dismissal for nought, the sort that barely troubles the scorebook but troubles the ego just fine. It's Laurie's 3rd duck of the season and the 7th of an increasingly seasoned career, edging him to a very respectable 141st of 1,399 in the all-time reckoning. Not bad company at all — Laurie now sits shoulder to shoulder on 7 career ducks with Jamie Ratcliffe, who racked up the same tally across 49 innings in his 2008–2011 stint and even bagged Duck of the Year in 2009 for his trouble. Laurie will be hoping to follow that trajectory, minus the runs, obviously. Week in Summary One duck from two fixtures — the rest of the LPCC top order showed uncharacteristic restraint and actually troubled the scorers. Consistency of a very particular kind eluded the wider membership this week, though Laurie flew the flag admirably. 🦆 Duck News — 27–31 August 2011

3 ducks
Week 20 · 27 Aug 2011 – 31 Aug 2011
Three matches, three ducks this week — a tidy, evenly-rationed haul for the exclusive zero club, one apiece and nobody being greedy about it. 2nd XI v Staplehurst CC (away) Thomas Beeney continued his season-long residency at the crease-and-back, caught behind off Tom Fuller (via Tom Henson) at number seven for his 5th duck of the season and 18th of a fine career — 63rd in the all-time table. A fully paid-up Debutante Duck (his very first senior innings went the same way) and a regular on the Ducks on the Road top 10, Thomas now sits level on 18 with 1988–2006 stalwart Russell Baldwin, kindred spirits four decades apart. His kin Michael Beeney, a parent-and-child duo, keeps the surname busy this week too — see below. Midweek XI v The Mote CC (away) Iain Rawson was bowled by Robert Adams at number six for his 3rd duck of the season and 15th career nought, edging up to 72nd all-time. On this very day in 1980, Allan Cooper made a duck of his own against Staplehurst CC, a name permanently enshrined in the club's Only Two Quacks wing. Rawson's sibling Glen isn't far behind on 11 career ducks, so the family clearly has an eye for the art of not scoring. Sunday 1st XI v Cowdrey CC (home) Michael Beeney was run out by Chris Jesson at number 10 without troubling the scorers, his 1st duck of the season and a hefty 33rd of his career — 28th all-time. A long-standing Diamond Duck club member (out without facing a ball, back in 2002) and one half of the Only Two Quacks pairing with Ian Brattle, Michael also carries a Retirement Duck credential from a past farewell. On this day in 1996, Daniel Thirkell made a duck of his own against Mullion — good company to keep. Week in summary Three matches, three ducks, one each — no repeats, no pairs, no king pairs, just steady contributions to the cause. The Beeney household quietly extends its grip on club duck output, parent and child both adding to the tally this week, albeit in entirely separate fixtures. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 🥇 Thomas Beeney — 5 ducks, 14 innings (35.71%) 🥈 Richard Piper — 5 ducks, 29 innings (17.24%) 🥉 Martin Harris — 4 ducks, 12 innings (33.33%) Richard Knowler — 4 ducks, 13 innings (30.77%) -Adam Wright — 3 ducks, 15 innings (20.0%) Thomas Beeney holds top spot, but Martin Harris's 33% strike rate suggests he's lurking with intent. Plenty of season left for the standings to shuffle. Next fixtures loom, and the ducks are surely just warming up. Keep the bats loose and the scorebooks ready — more nought-based news to follow. 🦆🏏0️⃣

Seven fixtures, six ducks, and one full ceremonial appearance of the honours board this week. The zero club has been busy. 🏏 Saturday — Lenham CC The 2nds' Thomas Beeney departed for his 4th duck of the campaign, caught by a fielder whose name — "Unsure" — rather summed up the whole affair, bowled through by Matthew Shrimpton. That's 17 career ducks now (rank 69 of 1399), pulling him level with Clive Newman, who required 370 innings between 1992 and 2010 to reach the same tally. Beeney remains a proud Debutante Duck alumnus and continues his climb up the Ducks on the Road top 10. 🌱 Sunday — Great Chart CC Martin Harris walked out and straight back again, lbw first ball to Darren McGoldrick — a golden duck 0️⃣ for the away 2nds. Adam Wright fell bowled by Gayashan Munasinghe, his 3rd duck of the season and 3rd career (rank 208), level with Daniel McCarthy (2006–2007). Then the ceremony the club exists for: a genuine Duck and Ducklings 🥇. Richard Knowler was golden ducked first ball, caught behind off George Brown, and was joined in the sheds moments later by son Joshua, caught off Lee Thompson having faced four. Richard's 4th of the season brings him to 7 career (rank 143, level with Jamie Ratcliffe), while Joshua's 2nd of the season takes him to 10 (rank 108, level with Jack Jenner, 2003–2008) and a proud member of the Duck of the Year 2010 roll. 🦆 Duck News — 13 to 17 August 2011

3 ducks
Week 18 · 13 Aug 2011 – 17 Aug 2011
Only three ducks slipped through the net this week, but as ever quality trumped quantity in the exclusive zero club. 🏏 Saturday 13 August — 1st XI away at Milstead CC Craig Sands became the week's opening statistic, caught and bowled by Ben Ryder for nought at first drop. It's his 3rd duck of the season and 17th of a long and storied career, moving him to 64th in the all-time standings from 323 innings. That tally puts him level with Clive Newman, who played 1992–2010 and needed 370 innings to reach the same unhappy total — Craig getting there rather more efficiently, if "efficiently" is the right word for accumulating noughts. 🕰️ Wednesday 17 August — Midweek XI at home to Tunbridge Wells Wednesday CC A rare mid-week double helping. Tim Wright opened the batting and the scoring column stayed resolutely shut, caught behind (fielder unsure, always the way) off S Collier. His 1st duck of the season nudges him to 6 for his career in 127 innings, drawing level with Laurie Probert, a 2008–2011 contemporary who reached the same mark in a rather brisker 79. A chip off the old block too — Tim's relative Adam Wright already carries 2 career ducks of his own, so the family clearly knows the drill. Down at number 10, Martin Harris continued his extraordinary campaign, caught off J- Knott without scoring. It's his 3rd duck of the season from just 8 innings — a frankly heroic 37.5% strike rate for failure — and his 5th career duck in 21 innings, ranking 171st of 1398 all time. That puts him alongside Iain Cardew, who played 1994–2011 and is himself a fully paid-up member of the Debutante Duck club. Martin, meanwhile, sits proudly as reigning Duck of the Year for 2011 — a crown he is defending with real conviction. Kin note: relative Jon Harris carries a mighty 90 career ducks, so the ability to leave a ball alone rather too well appears to run thick in the bloodline. 📋 Week in Summary Three ducks from five matches — the 1st XI and Midweek XI doing the honours between them, while the 2nd XI, 3rd XI and the remaining fixture came away disappointingly duck-free. Not a vintage week for the collectors, but a quietly excellent one for Martin Harris, who is now averaging comedy at a rate the rest of the club can only admire. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season Leaderboard (2011) - Richard Piper — 26 inn, 5 ducks, 19.23% - Martin Harris — 8 inn, 3 ducks, 37.5% - Richard Knowler — 10 inn, 3 ducks, 30.0% - Thomas Beeney — 10 inn, 3 ducks, 30.0% - Craig Sands — 20 inn, 3 ducks, 15.0% Martin Harris's strike rate remains the number to watch — at this pace the Duck of the Year title may need re-engraving before September. 🍩 Roll on the next fixture — the zero club never closes, and there's always a fresh nought waiting to be earned. The purest form of the achievement remains agonisingly out of reach, for now.
