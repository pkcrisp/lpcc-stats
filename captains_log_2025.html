Sun 21 Sep 2025 🧢 Friendly XIv Sherwood CC, Kent · HomeformWWLLW

Sunday brought Sherwood CC, Kent to Linton Park under grey but obliging skies, and having lost the toss I was informed, in no uncertain terms, that we would be fielding first. No matter — a captain adapts, and adapt we did.

Sherwood battled to 140 all out in 32.2 overs, Saad Sohail top-scoring with a well-crafted 62 that for a good while looked like carrying the game beyond us. But the lads chipped away superbly: Martin Harris was outstanding with the ball, snaring 3-20 and generally making life miserable for the Sherwood middle order, while I chipped in with 2-3 (I shall allow myself one mention of that, and no more) and Will Thew picked up a tidy 1-9. Lance Coventry and Patrick Minhard both departed without troubling the scorers — ducks, alas, being the great leveller of village cricket — but 140 was a total we felt we could chase.

And chase we did. Anthony Karassellos led the reply with a fine 35, well supported by Theo Clements's 26, and though the required rate nibbled at our nerves more than once, we crossed the line with four wickets in hand from our 25 overs. Elsewhere in the season's ledger, Luke Welsman continues to sit handsomely second in our Friendly XI run charts, and Will Thew now tops the wicket pile.

A tight, tense, thoroughly enjoyable afternoon — and a thoroughly good day for the club.

Sat 20 Sep 2025 🧢 Friendly XIv Cudham Wyse CC · HomeformWWWLL

Losing the toss on a warm, showery, blustery Saturday afternoon is rarely the omen you want, and so it proved when Cudham Wyse elected to bat and made themselves thoroughly at home. A one-wicket win, then — the kind that ages a captain by several years but tastes all the sweeter for it. Daniel now sits third in the season's run charts, and I find myself, somewhat improbably, top of the wicket-takers. Nerve-shredding stuff, but a fine win all the same. Onwards.
