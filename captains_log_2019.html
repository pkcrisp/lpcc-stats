Sun 22 Sep 2019 🧢 Sunday 1st XIv TC Tenterden CC · AwayformLTLWL

I toed the line hoping for a decent tea and a livelier finish than we got, but Tenterden had other ideas — and to be fair, they earned the draw. Martin Duffield gave us a proper battering with a superb 102, and James Allen chipped in a classy 77 to push them on to 252/4 from their 47 overs. To our credit the attack never gave up the ghost: Luke Welsman led the way with 1-24, Anthony Karassellos bowled beautifully tight for 1-32 with two maidens to his name, and I managed to prise one out myself for 1-35. We did at least send Guy Day back without troubling the scorers, which was some consolation on an afternoon when the showers seemed determined to have their say.

Chasing 253 was always going to be a stretch, but Alex Keeler set about it in style with a fine 75, and I was pleased to contribute 53 of my own alongside him. Archie Fairfax-Ross weighed in with a useful 27, and the extras column did its bit too with 35 — not something to shout about from the pavilion steps, but I'll take the runs however they come. Mark Christmas will want to forget his duck in a hurry, poor lad.

In the end we closed on 250/8 from our 41, five short of the target with the light and the overs against us. Honours shared, then, and on reflection a fair result — two sides giving as good as they got, and nobody quite able to land the killer blow.

Sat 21 Sep 2019 🧢 1st XIv Linden Park CC · HomeformWLLLW

I set fair, warm weather this Saturday against Linden Park, and once again the cricketing gods gave with one hand and took with the other. Aaron Hughes was in imperious form, 94 the standout of our innings, and he can consider himself unlucky not to have gone three figures better; Richard Piper chipped in a tidy 28 and Jordan Austin 26 as we closed on a very respectable 212/5 from our 45.2 overs. Linden Park closed on 216/6 from 44.1 overs, and the four runs proved just beyond our reach.

A word too for Aaron, who now sits third in the club's 1st XI run charts this season with 382 — reward for a summer of proper application.

So: beaten, not disgraced, and with plenty to admire in defeat. We dust ourselves down and go again next week.

Wed 18 Sep 2019 🧢 Midweek XIv LX Lord Cornwallis XI · HomeformWWLWW

Wednesday 18th September, the sun out and Lord Cornwallis XI in town — I won the toss and had no hesitation putting us in first. Turned out to be the right call, and no false modesty there. Richard Piper played a glorious innings for 76, and Aaron Hughes matched him for elegance with an unbeaten 64, the pair of them stitching together a 117-run stand for the third wicket that took the game away from the visitors before it had properly begun. Archie Fairfax-Ross chipped in a brisk 36, the extras column threw in a generous 40 of its own, and we declared our efforts at a handsome 241/4 from our 35 overs.

Defending that kind of total ought to be the easy part, though cricket rarely obliges. Norwood-Hill and Cornwallis put on a watchful 108 for the first wicket and for a while I confess I was pacing the boundary rather more than sitting comfortably in my chair. But we kept plugging away — I picked up 2-23 myself, with Luke Welsman matching me on 2-24, while Archie was simply magnificent again, snaring 2 for the princely cost of 1 run. Sir John Dunnington-Jefferson and E Shires both departed without troubling the scorers, which always raises a cheer from the field. Benjamin Austin had a torrid time behind the stumps, 12 byes going begging, but no matter — they folded for 203, and we walked off 38 runs to the good.

A fine win, and a proper afternoon's cricket. Onwards.

Sun 15 Sep 2019 🧢 1st XIv Linton Park CC · HomeformWWLLL
