🦆 Duck News — 21-22 September 1991

0 ducks
Week 22 · 21 Sep 1991 – 22 Sep 1991
A rare and troubling weekend for the Duck Club faithful: two fixtures played, two full days of honest endeavour from bat and ball, and not a single nought to show for it. 🏏 The dreaded quack proved elusive throughout — every batsman who walked to the crease this weekend contrived, infuriatingly, to get off the mark. Whether through good form, generous bowling, or a rare outbreak of competence up and down the order, the exclusive zero club gained no new inductees this period. Even our most reliable contributors to the art of not scoring were, this once, denied. Make no mistake — this is not cause for celebration in Duck Club circles. A blank weekend is simply 0 for 0, which even by our own standards is a disappointingly literal state of affairs. The committee notes this anomaly with concern and trusts it will be swiftly corrected. 0️⃣ Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 0️⃣ 1. Fred Cheesman — 6 ducks in 18 innings (33.33%) 2. Dave Charlton — 6 ducks in 28 innings (21.43%) 3. Piers McDonald — 5 ducks in 32 innings (15.63%) 4. Trevor Back — 4 ducks in 16 innings (25.0%) 5. Nick Stephens — 3 ducks in 14 innings (21.43%) Fred Cheesman continues to set the pace for Duck of the Year with a formidable 33% strike rate — genuine triumph through adversity, that. Dave Charlton snaps at his heels, while Trevor Back's 25% return from a mere 16 innings shows real efficiency in the art of not scoring, should he wish to close the gap. Rest assured, dear members, this drought is surely temporary. The season presses on, the fixtures keep coming, and somewhere out there a top edge, a leave-that-goes-astray, or a needless run-out is waiting to happen. Bats at the ready for next weekend — the ducks always come home to roost eventually. 🍩🦆

🦆 Duck News — 14–15 September 1991

1 duck
Week 21 · 14 Sep 1991 – 15 Sep 1991
Three fixtures on the books this weekend, and the harvest was slim — a single duck fell, but a proper vintage one at that. The other two matches were entirely fowl-free, a vegan weekend by Linton's usual standards. 🏏 Saturday 14th — 1st XI v Southborough CC (home) Batting at seven, Piers McDonald did the honourable thing and got it over with quickly, caught Ray Grayland off the bowling of G Lanham for nought. It's his 5th duck of the 1991 season and the 11th of an increasingly storied career, lodging him at 64th of 1162 in the all-time roll of dishonour. Piers remains a fixture of both the Lone Duck top 10 and the Tourist Duck top 10 — proof that whether home or away, in company or entirely alone at the crease, the result has a habit of rhyming. Neat historical footnote: on this very day back in 1958, Stan Wratten also trudged off for a duck, against Tunbridge Wells CC no less. Stan played from 1943 to 1984, racked up 35 ducks in the process, sits 14th all-time, and shares a "Quacking Together" board entry with the legendary John Ames. Some things about September just aren't meant to be. 🌱 Elsewhere The remaining two fixtures across the weekend passed without a single nought troubling the scorers — a rare and slightly suspicious outbreak of competence across the rest of the club. Make the most of it, it won't last. 📊 Week in Summary One duck from three matches is a quiet weekend by Duck Club standards, but quality over quantity — Piers has now put himself squarely into this season's shake-up, sitting third in the current standings. The chase for Duck of the Year continues to tighten. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Fred Cheesman — 6 ducks / 18 inns (33.3%) - Dave Charlton — 6 ducks / 26 inns (23.1%) - Piers McDonald — 5 ducks / 31 inns (16.1%) - Trevor Back — 4 ducks / 15 inns (26.7%) - Nick Stephens — 3 ducks / 14 inns (21.4%) Fred and Dave lead the way, but with the season deep into September, there's still time for a late surge. Piers, in particular, will fancy his chances of adding to his tally before the covers go on for winter. Next fixture, fresh scorecards, fresh opportunities for immortality of the zero-run variety. The Duck Club never sleeps — see you all next weekend. 🦆🍩0️⃣

🦆 Duck News — 7–11 September 1991

2 ducks
Week 20 · 7 Sep 1991 – 11 Sep 1991
A quiet week in the field — four matches, and only two men prepared to walk the lonely walk back to the pavilion. But what the week lacked in volume, it made up for in quality, with two Duck Hunting Season leaders both adding to their tallies on the very same weekend. 🏏 1st XI — away at Sandwich Town CC (Saturday) Dave Charlton, in at five, was bowled by Colin Aldred without troubling the scorers — his 6th duck of the season and 11th for his LPCC career, moving him to a healthy 60th of 1162 in the all-time standings from 66 innings. A member of a duck-inclined household too — his sibling Sean Charlton carries 4 career ducks of his own, so the trait clearly runs in the blood. Neat bit of calendar symmetry here: on this very day back in 1977, none other than club legend Tim Thirkell also made a duck, against St Just CC, on his way to a career haul of 64 and a lofty 4th all-time — Duck of the Year 1972, Full House, the lot. Good company to keep, even in defeat. 🌙 Midweek XI — away at The Mote CC Fred Cheesman departed for nought at number two, caught Nigel Mullarkey bowled Paul Heynes — his 6th duck of the season and a serious 23rd for his career, sitting 33rd of 1162 all-time from 107 innings. Fred is a certified Full House member (a duck at every position, 1 through 11) and, crucially, this is a live entry in his back-to-back Duck of the Year defence — he took the crown in both 1990 and 1991, and this latest effort keeps the trophy cabinet warm. He's also flying the family flag, with Steve and Stuart Cheesman both fellow paid-up members of the noughts club. On this day in 1954, Len Conway was doing much the same thing against Old Roffensians on his way to 12 career ducks — proof there's nothing new under the sun, or indeed under a golden duck-free sky. 📊 Week in summary Just the two ducks from four matches, but a notable coincidence — Dave Charlton and Fred Cheesman are now locked together at the very top of the season standings on 6 apiece. The Duck of the Year race for 1991 could not be tighter, and with Fred defending the title, Dave will fancy his chances of an upset. 🥇 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Fred Cheesman — 17 inns, 6 ducks (35.29%) - Dave Charlton — 24 inns, 6 ducks (25.0%) - Trevor Back — 14 inns, 4 ducks (28.57%) - Piers McDonald — 29 inns, 4 ducks (13.79%) - Nick Stephens — 14 inns, 3 ducks (21.43%) With the top two tied and the season drawing on, every innings from here counts double. Get down to the next fixture, take your seat, and watch history — or at least a nought — be made. 🦆🍩

🦆 Duck News — Week of 24 August 1991

3 ducks
Week 18 · 24 Aug 1991 – 30 Aug 1991
Only three ducks fell to the hunters this week, but what they lacked in volume they made up for in pedigree — every single one arriving courtesy of the 1st XI, three different matches, three grand old names on the club's all-time charts. The exclusive zero club keeps its standards high. 1st XI Saturday 24th, home to Petts Wood CC — Paul Bowles opened the innings and closed it just as briskly, caught Howard bowled Chisholm without scoring. It's his first duck of the season but his 47th for the club from 769 innings, holding steady at 7th of 1,158 in the all-time standings — rarefied company for a man who's already banked two Duck of the Year titles (1966, 1968), a fixture of the Ducks on the Road top 10, and one half of Only Two Quacks with Adam Pooley. On this very day in 1968, Benny Bell was doing the same against Aylesford Paper Mills CC, one of the 30 that carried him to 19th on the all-time list across a 1966–75 career. Sunday 25th, home to Old Anchorians CC — Dave Charlton, batting at 7, bowled by R Smith for his 5th duck of the summer and 10th of his Linton career in just 61 innings, ranking 66th of 1,158. Consistency of a very particular kind runs in the family — sibling Sean Charlton carries 4 career ducks of his own. On this day in 1976, Brian Piper also trudged off for nought against Gravesend CC, one of 19 across a 1973–91 career. Monday 26th, home to Bearsted CC — Nigel Thirkell, at first drop, bowled Darren Wheeler, his 3rd duck of the season and 46th of his career from 753 innings, 8th of 1,158 all time. The name should ring a bell — father John Thirkell sits top of the entire club's all-time list with 90, brother Tim carries 64, brother Simon 24 and kinsman Stuart 1, making this Bearsted Green's most decorated duck dynasty. Nigel already holds a Duck of the Year (1986), places in both the Homebody Duck and Tourist Duck top 10s, and shares Only Two Quacks with Jim Hall. Fittingly, on this exact day in 1979, it was brother Tim's turn to fall — also against Old Anchorians CC, one of the 64 that keep him 4th on the all-time roll. Week in summary Six matches, three ducks, one team monopolising the lot — the 2nd, 3rd and 4th XIs and the Sunday sides all got off unscathed this week, which either means good batting or a quiet week for the umpires. Fitting, too, that Dave Charlton's fifth of the season lands him straight into the leaderboard picture below. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard 1. Fred Cheesman — 5 ducks, 15 innings, 33.33% 2. Dave Charlton — 5 ducks, 19 innings, 26.32% 3. Trevor Back — 4 ducks, 12 innings, 33.33% 4. Piers McDonald — 4 ducks, 26 innings, 15.38% 5. Nick Stephens — 3 ducks, 14 innings, 21.43% Fred Cheesman and Trevor Back tied at the sharpest strike rate in the club — triumph through adversity indeed. The season race is far from settled, and with the Thirkell name already in the frame, next week promises more material for the honours board. See you at the crease — or, more likely, straight back to the pavilion. 🦆🏏0️⃣

🦆 Duck News — 17–21 August 1991

5 ducks
Week 17 · 17 Aug 1991 – 21 Aug 1991
Five ducks paddled into the record book this week across four fixtures, and the honours boards creak a little further under the weight. Saturday, 1st XI at Whitstable CC Nigel Thirkell departed at first drop, caught T Pattenden bowled Andy Robertson, his 2nd duck of the season and 45th of a career that now sits 8th in the all-time list of 1157. The parallel is closer to home than the calendar suggests — on this very day in 1963, his own father John Thirkell, the club's all-time No.1 with 90 to his name, also made a duck, against The Mote CC. Very much the family trade. Nigel remains a fixture of the Homebody and Tourist Duck top tens, plus a shared Duck of the Year crown from 1986. Saturday, 2nd XI at Bethersden CC A grim one-two for the lower order. Debutant Davidson opened his senior account and closed it in the same breath, bowled N Ward for nought on his very first outing — instant and unwanted membership of the Debutante Duck club. Moments later Richard Piper followed him back, bowled J Thompson, his 2nd duck of the season and 2nd of a young career now seven innings old, rank 266 of 1157 and already a name on the Full House, Homebody and Lone Duck top tens. His father Brian carries 19 career ducks of his own, so the ledger runs in the family. Both dismissals echo this same date in 1969, when Percy Brattle made his own duck against Mote A. Wednesday, Midweek XI at Gravesend CC Paul Putland enjoyed himself no end, removing two Linton batsmen in a row for nought. Fred Cheesman went first, caught D Gurton, his 5th duck of the season, stretching his lead at the top of the current standings, and 22nd of a career that already ranks him 36th of 1157 all time. Fred banked Duck of the Year honours in 1990, and on this form looks a fair bet to add 1991 to the cabinet too. In came debutant D Bray next, caught Glen Giddings off the very next over, a duck on debut to make it two Putland victims in short order. Both falls echo this same date in 1955, when John Ames made his against Halfway House. Week in summary Five ducks, four matches, and two of them doubles in the same innings — Bethersden and Gravesend both shipped a pair of wickets without a run troubling the scorers. Two brand new members welcomed to the Debutante Duck club in the same week, which is either coincidence or a worrying sign for the selectors. Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Fred Cheesman — 5 ducks / 14 inn (35.71%) - Trevor Back — 4 ducks / 12 inn (33.33%) - Dave Charlton — 4 ducks / 15 inn (26.67%) - Piers McDonald — 4 ducks / 24 inn (16.67%) - Nick Stephens — 3 ducks / 14 inn (21.43%) Fred tightens his grip on the crown, but there's a queue forming behind him. Same time next week — pads on, bats optional. 🦆🍩0️⃣

Another member of the 1958 fan club. - Rob Doick made no friends with the bowler, bowled first ball for a proper golden duck — his 3rd of the season and career alike, at just 16 innings a rapid ascent up the rankings. - Nick Stephens battled 9 balls before falling, his 3rd of the season, 4th career.
