LPCC Duck Report — 24-25 September 2022

6 ducks
Week 34 · 24 Sep 2022 – 25 Sep 2022
🦆 A rich weekend at Linton Park: six ducks across two fixtures, and one genuinely historic landmark reached along the way. 🏏 Saturday — 1st XI vs The Mote CC Nolan Wilde went lbw to Oliver Alexander-Frost at five, his 6th duck of the season and 10th for the club — level with Alex Keeler (2016-2022, 10 from 109), just one place above him at 121st all-time. A family trade too: Zach and Russell Wilde are both already club duck-holders. Theo Clements fell next, bowled by the same Alexander-Frost — more on him below, he wasn't done yet. Sam King followed at seven, also bowled by Alexander-Frost, his 4th duck for club and season. On this very day in 2003, Brian Piper made a duck against Penshurst Park on his way to 24 — fine historical company. And then the big one: at nine, Tim Thirkell was bowled by Matthew White for his 100th career duck — a full century of noughts, extending his lead at the very top of the all-time list (still comfortably 1st of 1531). The honours board already groans under Full House, Duck of the Year 1972, and top-ten spots in the Lone Duck and Homebody Duck charts. It runs in the family — father John on 90, brothers Nigel and Simon on 60 and 59 — a proper duck dynasty. 🌤 Sunday — 1st XI vs Crusaders CC Theo Clements walked out again and was bowled by S Whale — a grim pair for the weekend, Saturday's 3rd of the season followed inside 24 hours by his 4th. He joins Archie Fairfax-Ross (2018-2022, 3 from 33), and on this day in 1960 Len Conway made a duck against Linton Park CC on his way to 12 — scant comfort for a wretched weekend. John Karassellos was run out for his 5th of the season, moving to 35 career ducks — level with Jordan Austin (2008-2022, 35 from 256) — and stretching his own Ducks in a Row honour to five straight. Anthony and Michaela Karassellos keep it a family affair. 📊 Summary: six ducks, two matches, a century for the ages, and Oliver Alexander-Frost doing for three Linton batters single-handedly. Theo Clements will want this weekend back. 🏆 Duck Hunting Season leaderboard - Luke Welsman — 8 ducks, 38 innings (21.05%) - Nolan Wilde — 6 ducks, 33 innings (18.18%) - Thomas Beeney — 5 ducks, 14 innings (35.71%) - Toby Joseph — 5 ducks, 22 innings (22.73%) - John Karassellos — 5 ducks, 27 innings (18.52%) 🦆 Same time next week — the ducks never take a weekend off, and neither do we. 0️⃣🏏

He shares Quacking Together honours with Jim Hall, and the failing clearly runs in the blood — father John on 90, brothers Nigel (60) and Simon (59), cousins Daniel (33), Christopher (28) and Will (27) all fellow members in good standing. Fittingly, his parallel lands on the very same date as Carter's — Benny Bell, 1972, Staplehurst CC — two Linton men fifty years and ten batting positions apart, united in nought. 📋 The Week in Brief Just two ducks from four matches — a rare outbreak of restraint from the membership. All eyes now turn to Tim Thirkell, who arrives at the next fixture one failure away from the ton of ducks. History, of the most inglorious kind, awaits.
