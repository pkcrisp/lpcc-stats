Week 24 · 26 Sep 2009
A single fixture out on the park this week, and for once the exclusive zero club had absolutely nothing to report. Not a golden duck, not a king pair, not so much as a single nervous nought to log in the honours books. The ducks, it seems, evaded capture entirely. 1 duck
Week 23 · 19 Sep 2009 – 20 Sep 2009
Two fixtures on the go this weekend, and the ducks kept a low profile, offering the Club just a single casualty. Quality over quantity, evidently — Saturday's lot batted through unscathed, leaving Sunday to do all the work. Sunday 1st XI v Tenterden CC In at number four, Jamie Ratcliffe departed to a catch credited, magnificently, to "Unsure" off the bowling of Richard Pearman — out for nought, his 5th duck of the season and, remarkably, his 5th of his entire LPCC career too. Every single one of Jamie's ducks has come this very year, in just his 26th innings for the club, which puts him 159th of 1377 in the all-time standings. Jamie's arriving at the same destination considerably quicker, for better or worse. A word for the family tree while we're here — Jamie's sibling Paul Ratcliffe carries a single career duck of his own, evidently the more restrained of the two, at least so far. Week in Summary One duck from two matches — the flock mostly gave the slip this weekend, but Jamie Ratcliffe kept the books open for business. A quiet round by Duck Club standards, though the season race is very much alive.
