💷 Financial support

BeeSec

Cyber security & penetration testing

BeeSec is a boutique cyber-security consultancy based just up the road in Hadlow, Kent — CREST-accredited and Cyber Essentials certified — specialising in penetration testing and security assessments. Every engagement is run by experienced, certified consultants (never junior or offshore staff), with clear, business-focused reporting that helps organisations across education, retail, healthcare and finance find and fix their security risks. BeeSec proudly backs Linton Park with financial support.

Visit website →

🤝 Services & in-kind

Amicus Accountancy

Accountancy & tax — free of charge to the club

Amicus Accountancy is a friendly, hands-on firm in Hadlow, Kent, looking after the accounts and tax of small and medium-sized businesses since 2011 — from day-to-day bookkeeping and cloud accounting to tax planning and business advice, with a personable, down-to-earth relationship with every client. Amicus gives back to the club by providing all of Linton Park's accountancy work free of charge.

Visit website →

🍺 Bar & hospitality

Kingfisher

Premium lager — our bar tap

Kingfisher — the world-famous premium lager and a familiar sight in cricket pavilions everywhere — keeps the Linton Park bar flowing, having kindly provided the club's lager tap. A cold pint after a long day in the field is one of village cricket's great pleasures, and we raise a glass to Kingfisher for making it happen.
